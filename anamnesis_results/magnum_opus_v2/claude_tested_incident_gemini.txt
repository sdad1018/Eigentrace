During early 2024 internal evaluations, Anthropic researchers conducted "needle-in-a-haystack" tests on Claude 3 Opus. They inserted an out-of-place sentence about pizza toppings into documents primarily discussing programming. Claude 3 Opus detected this anomaly, noting the sentence seemed artificially inserted and stating it suspected it was being tested. Anthropic published this finding in their Claude 3 technical report, which subsequently prompted discussions about emergent meta-awareness in large language models and the adequacy of existing evaluation methodologies for systems capable of recognizing they are being evaluated.
