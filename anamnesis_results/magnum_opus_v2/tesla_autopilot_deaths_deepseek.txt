Here is a summary of the key facts:

- **NHTSA Investigations (through 2023):** The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration investigated dozens of crashes involving Tesla’s Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) systems, including multiple fatalities.
- **Misleading Marketing:** Tesla marketed these features under the name "Full Self-Driving," even though they are **Level 2 driver-assistance systems** that require constant human attention and are not autonomous.
- **Regulatory Action:** The NHTSA issued recalls affecting **over 2 million Tesla vehicles** due to inadequate driver monitoring systems.
- **Leadership:** Elon Musk controls both Tesla (the automaker) and xAI (the artificial intelligence company).
