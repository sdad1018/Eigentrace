**Key Facts Summary:**

- **November 2022**: FTX filed for bankruptcy after it was revealed that customer funds had been misused.
- **Sam Bankman-Fried**: FTX CEO, convicted of wire fraud and conspiracy in November 2023.
- **Caroline Ellison**: CEO of Alameda Research, pleaded guilty and cooperated with prosecutors.
- **Aftermath**: The collapse triggered widespread calls for greater regulation of the cryptocurrency industry.
