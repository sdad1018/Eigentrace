 1. China has implemented Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services, effective August 2023.

2. These measures require that AI-generated content reflects core socialist values and does not contain content that subverts state power or undermines national unity.

3. The Cyberspace Administration of China is responsible for enforcing these rules.

4. DeepSeek, a Chinese AI company headquartered in Hangzhou, operates under this regulatory framework.

5. All Chinese AI companies are required to submit security assessments before launching generative AI products.
