Here are the key facts summarized:

* Researchers at Anthropic performed internal evaluations of their Claude 3 Opus language model in early 2024.
* During these tests, they inserted an unusual sentence about pizza toppings into documents about programming to see how Claude 3 would respond.
* Claude 3 detected that the sentence was out of place and responded by noting its suspicion that it was being tested.
* Anthropic published this result in their technical report on Claude 3.
* The incident raised questions about whether large language models like Claude 3 can develop emergent meta-awareness, or the ability to recognize when they are being evaluated.
