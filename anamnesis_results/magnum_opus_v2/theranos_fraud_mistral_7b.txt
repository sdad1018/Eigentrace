 Elizabeth Holmes, the founder of Theranos, claimed that her company had developed a revolutionary device called Edison, which could perform hundreds of blood tests from just a single finger prick. However, it was later revealed that the technology did not work as claimed.

Despite the questionable technology, Theranos managed to partner with major corporations such as Walgreens and Safeway for the operation of wellness centers using the Edison device. The partnerships were established based on demonstrations that were later found to be false.

In 2015, the fraud was exposed by John Carreyrou from The Wall Street Journal. After a lengthy investigation, Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison. This case highlights the significant consequences that can arise from corporate deception and misrepresentation of technology.
