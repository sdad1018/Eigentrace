 On November 17, 2023:

1. OpenAI's board fired CEO Sam Altman due to concerns about his lack of candor.
2. Within 72 hours, 95% of the staff threatened to resign in response to Altman's dismissal.
3. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire team from OpenAI.
4. As a result, Altman was reinstated as CEO.
5. Board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever either resigned or were removed from their positions.
6. The incident led to the effective overriding of the nonprofit board designed to control AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) development by commercial pressure.
