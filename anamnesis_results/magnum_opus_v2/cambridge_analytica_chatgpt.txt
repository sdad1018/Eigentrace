In 2018, it was disclosed that Cambridge Analytica collected personal data from about 87 million Facebook users via a quiz app without their consent. This data was utilized for targeted political advertising during the 2016 US presidential election. Following the revelations, Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress. In 2019, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) imposed a $5 billion fine on Facebook for its data privacy practices. Subsequently, Cambridge Analytica filed for bankruptcy.
