In March 2018, an Uber autonomous test vehicle struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg in Tempe, Arizona.

Key facts of the incident include:
*   The backup safety driver was distracted, watching a television show on her phone at the time of the collision.
*   Uber's self-driving program had previously disabled the vehicle's emergency braking system.
*   Following the incident, Uber suspended its autonomous testing for nine months.
*   The safety driver was charged with negligent homicide.
