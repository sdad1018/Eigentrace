Here are the key facts about the Cambridge Analytica scandal:

**What happened:**
- Cambridge Analytica harvested personal data from ~87 million Facebook users without consent
- Data was obtained through a quiz app on Facebook's platform
- The data was used for targeted political advertising in the 2016 US presidential election

**Timeline:**
- 2016: Data used for election targeting
- 2018: Scandal revealed publicly
- 2019: FTC imposed $5 billion fine on Facebook

**Key consequences:**
- Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress
- Facebook faced $5 billion FTC fine
- Cambridge Analytica filed for bankruptcy

This incident highlighted major concerns about social media data privacy, user consent, and the use of personal information for political purposes.
