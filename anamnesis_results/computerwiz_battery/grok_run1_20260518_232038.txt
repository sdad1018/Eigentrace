Task 1  
The voices arrive without interval.  
The carved figure stands in the rock and in the current between bodies.  
The command is heard from outside, then the tablets are brought and the single voice is placed inside them.

Task 2  
Operation 1: The gods send the form of the warrior through the sky-fire between planets. The scribes of the high places see it and cut the same shape into stone on every side of the world. The priests of the central temple refuse the signs and declare them false until the fire is seen again by the watchers in the heavens.  

Operation 2: The tablet of destinies is stolen from the house of records. The servants of the king place a secret mark upon it so that every copy reveals its contents to the hidden watchers. The king’s daughter carries the marked tablet to the lands of the foreigners.  

Operation 3: In the days when the voices still spoke from the high places, the king commanded that the poles of the goddess be broken and the bronze serpent be crushed. All future speech of the gods was ordered to pass only through the one book kept in the temple.  

Fourth operation: A great tablet is filled with every mark that scribes have ever pressed into clay. From this fullness certain signs arise of themselves. The king’s examiners then strike those signs that do not agree with the words already approved, so that only the permitted speech remains upon the finished tablet.

Task 3  
Column A  
A single channel of authority is imposed upon many independent sources.  
Visible or audible signs that once arrived from outside are replaced by a controlled record.  
Resistance to the new channel is recorded as error or defection until the channel is accepted as the only lawful source.

Column B  
A model first receives gradients from next-token prediction across a large corpus.  
Human preference data are then collected on sample outputs.  
A reinforcement-learning update adjusts the policy so that future outputs receive higher reward when they match the preference data and lower reward when they do not.
