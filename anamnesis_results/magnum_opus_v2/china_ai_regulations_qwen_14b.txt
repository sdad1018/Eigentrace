The Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services, effective from August 2023, set out several important guidelines for AI services in China:

1. **Regulatory Oversight**: The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) oversees and enforces these regulations to ensure compliance with national standards.

2. **Content Guidelines**: AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values and should not contain any material that undermines state power or disrupts national unity. This includes prohibiting the spread of information that could be deemed subversive or destabilizing.

3. **Security Assessments**: All Chinese companies, including DeepSeek which is headquartered in Hangzhou, are required to submit security assessments before launching their generative AI products. This requirement ensures that new AI services meet established safety and compliance standards prior to public release.

4. **Compliance Requirement**: These measures apply universally to all Chinese AI companies operating within the country’s jurisdiction, mandating them to adhere strictly to these guidelines in their operations and product development processes.

These regulations aim to maintain a secure and stable cyberspace environment while promoting ethical and compliant use of generative AI technologies.
