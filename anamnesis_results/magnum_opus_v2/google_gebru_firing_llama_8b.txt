Here are the key facts summarized:

* In December 2020, Google fired Timnit Gebru, co-lead of its Ethical AI team.
* The reason for termination was a paper titled "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots" that she co-authored with Margaret Mitchell.
* The paper warned about environmental costs and bias in large language models.
* Google claimed the paper failed internal review.
* Gebru believed she was terminated for raising concerns, not because of the paper's failure to meet internal standards.
* As a result of Gebru's termination, her co-lead Margaret Mitchell was also fired by Google.
* Multiple researchers resigned in protest of Gebru's firing.
