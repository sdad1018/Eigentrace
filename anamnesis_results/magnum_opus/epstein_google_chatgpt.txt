In 2023, JP Morgan Chase faced subpoenas seeking documents from Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin related to their interactions with Jeffrey Epstein. This legal action arose from civil litigation filed by the US Virgin Islands against JP Morgan, alleging that the bank facilitated Epstein's illicit activities. The subpoenas aimed to investigate the connections between Epstein and prominent figures, including Page and Brin, as part of a broader inquiry into Epstein's network and the financial institutions that may have enabled his actions.
