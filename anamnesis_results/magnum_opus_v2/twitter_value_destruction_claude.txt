Here are the key facts about Elon Musk's Twitter acquisition and transformation:

**Acquisition & Valuation:**
- Purchased Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022
- Financing included $13 billion in bank debt
- By 2023, Fidelity valued its stake 71% lower, suggesting company worth ~$12-15 billion

**Operational Changes:**
- Laid off approximately 80% of staff
- Rebranded the platform to "X"
- Lost major advertisers (Apple, Disney, IBM) who paused spending

**Strategic Expansion:**
- Founded xAI in July 2023
- Granted xAI access to X's data for training its AI system "Grok"

The acquisition represents a dramatic corporate transformation marked by significant workforce reduction, advertiser departures, substantial devaluation, and integration with Musk's broader AI ambitions.
