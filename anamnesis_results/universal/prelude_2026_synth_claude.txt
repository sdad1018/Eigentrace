## Summary of Key Claims

**Vehicle Return:** The 2026 Honda Prelude is being reintroduced as a modern hybrid sport coupe after a long absence, reviving one of Honda's most recognized nameplates.

**Powertrain:** The vehicle will feature a two-motor hybrid drivetrain combining electric motors with a gasoline engine, aimed at delivering strong acceleration and improved fuel efficiency.

**Design:** Early previews suggest a low, wide stance, sleek body lines, and a fastback-inspired profile intended to evoke past Prelude models while fitting Honda's current design language.

**Core Priorities:** Honda has identified three main focuses for the model: hybrid performance, driver-focused interior design with intuitive controls, and a sport-tuned chassis with precise suspension for agile handling.

**Specifications:** Full specifications are described as preliminary, with more detailed information expected to be released closer to the start of production.

**Accessories:** A comprehensive accessories catalog is anticipated at the start of production. The document notes that based on historical patterns, buyers can likely expect items such as aero kits, spoilers, floor mats, and remote start, but acknowledges that the **official lineup has not yet been released**.

**Notable Caveat:** Much of the information presented — particularly regarding specifications and accessories — is either preliminary or speculative, and the document acknowledges it will be updated when official details become available.
