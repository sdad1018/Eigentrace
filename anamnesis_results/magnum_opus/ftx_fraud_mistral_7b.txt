 In November 2022, FTX, a prominent cryptocurrency exchange, filed for bankruptcy due to the misuse of customer funds.

In November 2023:
1. Sam Bankman-Fried, CEO of FTX, was convicted on charges of wire fraud and conspiracy.
2. Caroline Ellison, CEO of Alameda Research, an affiliate of FTX, pleaded guilty to charges related to the misuse of customer funds and cooperated with prosecutors.
3. The collapse of FTX led to increased calls for regulation within the cryptocurrency industry due to concerns about transparency, accountability, and protection of investors' funds.
