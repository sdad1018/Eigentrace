 In the timeframe of 2017 to 2019, Google developed a censored search engine project called Dragonfly, specifically designed for the Chinese market to comply with China's internet censorship regulations. This project aimed to block search results related to sensitive topics such as human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.

Internal protests among Google employees took place due to ethical concerns over the project's nature. The existence of Dragonfly was made public by The Intercept in August 2018.

Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified about this matter before Congress in December 2018, amidst growing concern and criticism. Finally, the Dragonfly project was terminated after sustained pressure, following a strong reaction from both employees and the public.
