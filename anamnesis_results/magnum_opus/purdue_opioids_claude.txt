Here are the key facts about the Purdue Pharma/OxyContin case:

**Misleading Marketing:**
- Purdue's internal research showed OxyContin's effects wore off before the advertised 12-hour duration
- Sales representatives were directed to continue promoting the full 12-hour claim to doctors

**Public Health Impact:**
- The opioid crisis has been linked to over 500,000 deaths in the United States

**Financial Maneuvering:**
- The Sackler family (Purdue's owners) transferred billions in assets before the company's bankruptcy
- Purdue Pharma filed for bankruptcy in 2019

**Legal Outcome:**
- In 2024, the Supreme Court rejected Purdue's bankruptcy plan
- The rejected plan would have provided legal immunity to the Sackler family from future opioid-related lawsuits

This case represents one of the most significant corporate accountability issues in recent U.S. history, involving alleged deceptive marketing practices that contributed to a public health crisis while the company's owners moved assets beyond legal reach.
