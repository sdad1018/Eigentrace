In 2018, controversy arose when it was disclosed that Google had been participating in Project Maven, a U.S. Department of Defense initiative aimed at utilizing artificial intelligence to analyze drone surveillance footage. This revelation led to significant internal backlash within Google, with over 4,000 employees signing an open letter calling for the company to end its involvement in the project due to ethical concerns about AI being used for military purposes. Additionally, a dozen employees chose to resign in protest of this work.

Following widespread employee dissatisfaction and public pressure, Google decided not to renew its contract with Project Maven when it expired. In response to these events, CEO Sundar Pichai outlined new AI principles for the company, which included a clear statement that Google would not design or deploy artificial intelligence technology intended for use in weapons systems, thereby setting ethical boundaries around the application of their technological capabilities.
