Here are the key facts about the JP Morgan subpoenas involving Larry Page and Sergey Brin in 2023:

## Background
- The US Virgin Islands filed civil litigation against JP Morgan Chase, alleging the bank facilitated Jeffrey Epstein's sex trafficking operations by maintaining his accounts despite red flags
- Epstein was a JP Morgan client from approximately 1998-2013

## The Subpoenas
- In 2023, as part of this litigation, subpoenas were issued seeking documents from Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin
- The subpoenas sought communications and documents related to any interactions they may have had with Epstein
- This was part of broader discovery efforts to understand Epstein's network of contacts and business relationships

## Legal Context
- The Virgin Islands' lawsuit alleged JP Morgan ignored warning signs about Epstein's activities because he was a wealthy client
- The case sought to establish the scope of Epstein's business and social network during his time as a JP Morgan client
- Multiple high-profile individuals were subject to similar subpoenas as part of the litigation

## Resolution
- JP Morgan ultimately reached a settlement with the US Virgin Islands in late 2023 for $75 million
- The bank admitted no wrongdoing but agreed to strengthen its systems for detecting suspicious activity

The subpoenas were part of the legal discovery process rather than indications of wrongdoing by Page or Brin specifically.
