Here are the key facts about the FTX collapse:

**Timeline & Events:**
- November 2022: FTX filed for bankruptcy following revelation of customer fund misuse
- November 2023: Sam Bankman-Fried convicted on wire fraud and conspiracy charges

**Key Players:**
- Sam Bankman-Fried: FTX CEO, convicted of criminal charges
- Caroline Ellison: CEO of Alameda Research, pleaded guilty and became cooperating witness

**Core Issue:**
- Misuse of customer funds was the central problem that triggered the collapse

**Broader Impact:**
- The scandal intensified calls for cryptocurrency industry regulation

This represents one of the most significant corporate failures in the cryptocurrency sector, highlighting governance issues and the need for stronger oversight in digital asset markets.
