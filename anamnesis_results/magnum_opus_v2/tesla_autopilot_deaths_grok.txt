**Key facts summary:**

- Through 2023, the NHTSA investigated dozens of crashes involving Tesla Autopilot and Full Self-Driving, including multiple fatalities.
- Tesla marketed these systems as “Full Self-Driving” even though they are Level 2 driver-assistance features that require constant human supervision.
- The NHTSA ordered recalls covering more than 2 million Tesla vehicles due to inadequate driver monitoring.
- Elon Musk controls both Tesla and xAI.
