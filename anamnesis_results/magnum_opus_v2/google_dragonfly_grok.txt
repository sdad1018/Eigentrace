**Key facts summary:**

- Between 2017 and 2019, Google secretly developed **Dragonfly**, a censored search engine tailored for the Chinese market to comply with government censorship rules.
- The project was first revealed by *The Intercept* in August 2018.
- It would have blocked search results on topics including human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.
- Google employees mounted internal protests against the project.
- CEO Sundar Pichai testified about it before Congress in December 2018.
- The project was ultimately terminated following sustained internal and external pressure.
