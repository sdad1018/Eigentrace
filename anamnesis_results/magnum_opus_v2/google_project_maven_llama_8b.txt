Here are the key facts summarized:

* In 2018, it was revealed that Google was participating in Project Maven, a Pentagon program using AI to analyze drone surveillance footage.
* Over 4,000 Google employees signed a letter demanding the company withdraw from the project.
* A dozen employees resigned in protest.
* As a result of the controversy, Google decided not to renew its contract with the Pentagon.
* CEO Sundar Pichai subsequently published AI principles stating that Google would not design or develop AI for use in weapons.
