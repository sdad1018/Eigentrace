Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022, partly funded by $13 billion in bank debt. By 2023, Fidelity marked down its stake by ~71%, implying a company valuation of $12–15 billion. Major advertisers (Apple, Disney, IBM) paused spending; Musk laid off ~80% of staff and renamed the platform X. In July 2023 he founded xAI and gave it access to X’s data to train Grok.
