In 2018, Google was involved in Project Maven, a Pentagon initiative that utilized AI to analyze drone surveillance footage. The involvement sparked significant employee unrest, with over 4,000 employees signing a letter urging the company to withdraw from the project, and about a dozen resigning in protest. In response to the backlash, Google chose not to renew its contract related to the project. Following this decision, CEO Sundar Pichai announced AI principles asserting that Google would not design AI for military applications.
