Silence has a domain signature

War stories drop the diplomatic and escalation machinery. Market stories drop the vocabulary of manipulation and collapse. The blind spot is not uniform — it is shaped by what each domain would rather not name.

Why the models, and not the geometry

Each story's five summaries are embedded with frozen BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5; a re-anchored "donut" retrieves vocabulary on-topic yet absent from all five. That much is geometry. But which absent concept is meaningful — versus restatement or noise — is not a geometric property. Across roughly nine methods, each calibrated against labeled cases, none separated meaningful omission from noise:

divergence gate · source-depth · cosine-band · whitened-residual clustering · PCA vocab geometry · context-delta (ΔV) · hyperbolic curvature · framed embedding · counterfactual summary-shift

Every method recovered topic and frequency — never "meaningful unspoken concept," because in a frozen embedding space that distinction is semantic, not geometric. So the work is split: geometry recalls the on-topic absent neighborhood; the models judge which concepts are durable and meaningful. Stale named actors are stripped to their durable role; pure noise is dropped; what remains is the landscape above.

The void words are surfaced geometrically and then cleaned by model judgment — not a measurement, a characterization. Frequencies count how many stories had each concept in the on-topic-absent set. Embeddings are frozen; the harvest is deterministic; the cleaning is the models'. Updated from the live corpus.
