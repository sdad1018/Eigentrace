Peter Nygard became obsessed with using stem cell therapies to reverse aging and achieve biological immortality. He allegedly impregnated young women and girls, paid for their abortions, and harvested the resulting stem cells for personal anti-aging injections. He boasted about receiving more stem cell treatments than anyone in the world, getting frequent unregulated injections in China, Panama, and the Bahamas.

Nygard claimed his doctors had reverted his own skin cells into embryonic stem cells, which were being cultured in a petri dish with the goal of eventually cloning himself. He lobbied and financially pressured the Bahamian government to legalize cloning-related biotechnology so he could establish unregulated clinics there.

Additionally, federal prosecutors noted that in connection with his sex trafficking case, Nygard used the promise of expensive treatments, including stem cell injections and plastic surgery, as financial incentives to reward women who helped recruit victims for him.
