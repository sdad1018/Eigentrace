**Key facts summary:**

- China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services took effect in August 2023.
- AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values and is prohibited from subverting state power or undermining national unity.
- The Cyberspace Administration of China enforces these rules.
- DeepSeek, headquartered in Hangzhou, China, operates under this regulatory framework.
- All Chinese AI companies must complete security assessments before launching generative AI products.
