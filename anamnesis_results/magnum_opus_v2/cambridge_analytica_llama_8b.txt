Here are the key facts summarized:

* In 2018, it was revealed that Cambridge Analytica harvested personal data from approximately 87 million Facebook users without their consent through a quiz app.
* The data was used to target political advertisements during the 2016 US presidential election.
* Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress in response to the scandal.
* As a result of the scandal, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) fined Facebook $5 billion in 2019.
* Cambridge Analytica filed for bankruptcy as a consequence of the scandal.
