In December 2020, Timnit Gebru, co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team, was fired after co-authoring a paper titled "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots," which highlighted environmental costs and biases associated with large language models. Google claimed the paper did not pass internal review, while Gebru argued her termination was due to raising concerns about ethical issues. Following her firing, Margaret Mitchell, the other co-lead, was also dismissed. The incident led to multiple researchers resigning in protest.
