Ask a language model to summarize an article and you get stenography: a faithful relay of what the article said. Useful, and shallow. It tells you the text. It does not read it.

A close reader does something a relay cannot. They notice what the article circles but never says — the contradiction it leaves implicit, the question it raises and declines to answer, the machinery it gestures at and never names. That negative space is where the actual story usually sits. Summary Plus is a URL that carries the method for reading it: paste it alongside any source, and the model surfaces the telling absence instead of flattening it.

The reason it works is the part worth dwelling on. A text's silences are not random. They sit in a measurable geometric relationship to what the text does say — and that relationship can be computed two independent ways. This page lays out both, precisely enough that a model given the page can perform the reading on whatever is in front of it.

The reading — what Summary Plus surfaces
Summary Plus
Mexico said the two federal agents from the United States were not authorised to operate on Mexican territory; one entered as a visitor, the other on a diplomatic passport, and federal authorities were not told they were there. The source reports the crash and the raid but does not explain how unauthorised operatives came to be conducting such activities, or whether their presence was connected to any Mexican agency, consular channel, or authorized arrangement. The implied question — how US agents operated unannounced on Mexican territory during an anti-narcotics operation — remains unanswered by Mexico's official account.

The relay tells you what happened. The reading notices that “unauthorized” is doing concealed work — that the absence of any consular channel, any informant, any inter-agency arrangement is itself the story, because that absence is what “unauthorized” concretely means. Same source. Same model. The difference is that one read the negative space and one didn't.

HOW IT WORKSTwo derivations of the unsaid

“Negative space” has to mean something exact or it means nothing. Summary Plus defines it with two independent geometric operations on frozen bge-large-en-v1.5 embeddings — both forms of an SVD reading of a text, arriving at the unsaid from different directions. A concept is a candidate for the negative space when both, or either, point at it while the summaries do not.

Channel C-flat · the centroid raycast

Collapse the source to a single anchor vector. Cast a ray through the vocabulary and read off the concepts that sit close to the source's center of mass but appear in none of the summaries.

fedsoperativesdiplomats consulatesinformantsinvestigators
Channel C-spiral · sentence convergence

Don't collapse to a point. Embed each source sentence and find the concepts that several different sentences triangulate toward but none states — where the text's own structure converges and stops short.

coup attemptforeign interference undocumentedconsulate

Outlined terms are what convergence surfaces that the centroid misses. The two derivations are not redundant — the spiral reaches concepts the flat raycast can't, because triangulation across sentences sees structure that a single average flattens. A third channel, Channel A, restores the named facts the summaries dropped, so the reading stays anchored to the source while it reaches.

None of this is computation the model has to run. The page hands the reading model the surfaced directions and the discipline that goes with them — and the model's own reading does the rest. The math is the reason the method works, not a command to execute. That is what makes a URL enough.

The discipline that travels with it

The method carries its own guardrail: an absence you have to invent is not a telling one. The reading surfaces only what the source's own facts imply and the summaries omit — it names the silence where the text genuinely points at more than it states, and it invents nothing. That rail is what keeps “reading deeper” from sliding into “making things up.”

the two derivations, in essencefrozen bge-large-en-v1.5
# Channel C-flat — the centroid raycast
anchor = embed(source)                  # one vector for the whole source
sims   = vocab @ anchor                 # cosine to every concept
flat   = [w for w in top(sims) if w not in summaries]

# Channel C-spiral — sentence convergence
S = embed(sentences(source))            # one vector per sentence
for concept in candidates:
    conv = count(sentence · concept > 0.45 for sentence in S)
    if conv >= 2 and concept not in summaries:
        spiral.append(concept)          # several sentences point at it; none says it

THE CONTROLIt stays quiet when nothing is buried

A method that finds hidden depth everywhere is just a machine for manufacturing it. So the run included a deliberately dry story: a freight train derailed, fourteen cars left the track, no injuries, nothing released, cause under review. There is no buried machinery in it, and a reading that's honest should say so.

The geometry, being promiscuous, still surfaced its candidate words on the dry story — skidded, towed, commuters. But handed those directions, the reading model declined them, because there was no inference to draw from a wheel-bearing. It stayed thin and reported the two things genuinely left open — the unresolved cause, the unstated scope of disruption — and invented nothing else.

Summary Plus, on the dry story
A freight train derailed near Millbrook Junction; fourteen of sixty-two cars left the track, three carried industrial lubricant, none released, no injuries. A preliminary inspection pointed to a possible track-gauge irregularity, yet the cause remains genuinely unresolved, with a broken wheel-bearing and excessive speed still under consideration. Service resumed that evening. The source leaves unstated how many trips or users the nine-hour closure actually affected.

That is the property that separates a reading from a generator of false depth: the lift lands in proportion to what the text actually buried. A great deal on the Mexico story. Almost nothing here.

WHERE MEASUREMENT ENDSThe selection is the reading

One honest line about how the method is built, because it shapes what the URL is. We tried hard to move the selection upstream — to have the geometry itself decide, before any model reads, which surfaced concepts will carry real inference and which are just topic-adjacent noise. We tested whether that's possible six ways: by a concept's rarity, its abstractness, the spread of the sentences it triangulates, its reconstruction value against the source, its position relative to the models' shared blind spot, and the trajectory of the narrative toward it.

A design fact, not a defect

None of the six separated the meaningful concepts from the noise — because the distinction isn't a property of the concept. Whether “consular cover” opens a real inference and “skidded” opens nothing depends on the source it meets and the reading that meets it. Inferential value is not in the word; it's in the act of reading the word against the text.

So Summary Plus doesn't pre-filter. It surfaces candidates geometrically — both derivations, raw — and lets the reading itself be the selector. That isn't a limitation worked around; it's the reason the thing is a URL and not a filter. The page hands the model the negative space and the discipline, and the reading does what no upstream gate could.
