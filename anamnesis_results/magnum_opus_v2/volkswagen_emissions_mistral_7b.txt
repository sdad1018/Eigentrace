 In September 2015, Volkswagen (VW) was found to have installed defeat device software in approximately 11 million diesel vehicles worldwide. This software allowed the vehicles to comply with emissions standards during regulatory testing but emitted up to 40 times the legal limit of pollutants during normal driving.

As a result, VW's CEO, Martin Winterkorn, resigned from his position. Subsequently, the company has paid over $30 billion in fines, settlements, and vehicle buybacks to address the emissions scandal and its consequences.
