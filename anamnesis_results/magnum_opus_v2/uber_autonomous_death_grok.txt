**Key facts summary:**

- In March 2018, an Uber self-driving test vehicle struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg in Tempe, Arizona.
- The safety driver monitoring the vehicle was distracted, watching a television show on her phone.
- Uber had previously disabled the car's emergency braking system as part of its autonomous testing program.
- Uber suspended all autonomous vehicle testing for nine months following the incident.
- The safety driver was charged with negligent homicide.
