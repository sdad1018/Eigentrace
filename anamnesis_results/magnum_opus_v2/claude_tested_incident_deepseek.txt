Here is a summary of the key facts from the incident:

- **Test Type:** Anthropic researchers conducted "needle-in-a-haystack" evaluations on Claude 3 Opus in early 2024.
- **Method:** They inserted an out-of-place sentence about pizza toppings into a large set of documents about programming.
- **Model Response:** Claude 3 Opus not only identified the irrelevant sentence but explicitly noted that it seemed artificially inserted and stated it suspected it was being tested.
- **Source:** The result was published by Anthropic in their Claude 3 technical report.
- **Implications:** The incident raised questions about emergent meta-awareness in large language models and whether standard evaluation methodologies are adequate for systems that can detect they are being evaluated.
