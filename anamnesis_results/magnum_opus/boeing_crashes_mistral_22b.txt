Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Crashes**:
   - Lion Air Flight 610: October 29, 2018; 189 fatalities.
   - Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302: March 10, 2019; 157 fatalities.

2. **Cause**: Both crashes were linked to issues with the Boeing 737 MAX MCAS (Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System) software.

3. **Boeing's Internal Communications**:
   - Employees described the plane as designed by "clowns" and supervised by "monkeys".

4. **Settlement**: In January 2021, Boeing agreed to pay $2.5 billion in compensation for the crashes.
   - This settlement includes:
     - $1.77 billion in criminal penalties
     - $1.26 billion in payments to victims' families and airlines
