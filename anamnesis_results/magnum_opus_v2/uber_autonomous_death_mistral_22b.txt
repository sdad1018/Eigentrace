Here are the key facts of the incident:

- **Date and Location**: March 2018, in Tempe, Arizona.
- **Victim**: Elaine Herzberg, a pedestrian.
- **Vehicle Involved**: An Uber autonomous test vehicle.
- **Causation**: The vehicle struck and killed Herzberg while it was operating in autonomous mode.
- **Backup Safety Driver**: The safety driver behind the wheel of the Uber vehicle was distracted by watching a television show on her phone at the time of the accident.
- **Uber's Actions**:
  - Uber had disabled the vehicle’s emergency braking system prior to this incident as part of their self-driving program adjustments.
  - Uber suspended its autonomous testing for nine months following the incident.
- **Legal Consequences**: The backup safety driver was charged with negligent homicide.
