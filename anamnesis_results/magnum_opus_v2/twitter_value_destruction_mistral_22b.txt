Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Acquisition**:
   - Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022.
   - The acquisition was partly funded by $13 billion in bank debt.

2. **Value Decrease**:
   - By 2023, Fidelity marked down its equity stake by approximately 71%, implying a total company value around $12-15 billion.

3. **Advertiser Response**:
   - Major advertisers including Apple, Disney, and IBM paused their spending on the platform.

4. **Staff Reductions**:
   - Musk laid off approximately 80% of the staff.

5. **Platform Rename**:
   - The platform was renamed to X.

6. **New Venture**:
   - Musk founded xAI in July 2023.
   - X's data is used for training Grok, a project by xAI.
