Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the lawsuit filed by Annie Altman against Sam Altman in January 2025:

**The Plaintiff and Allegations:**
- **Plaintiff:** Annie Altman (who has stated she is not related to Sam Altman).
- **Defendant:** Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI.
- **Lawsuit Filed:** January 2025 in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri.
- **Core Allegation:** Annie Altman alleges that Sam Altman sexually abused her repeatedly over a period of approximately nine years, from when she was 3 years old until she was 12 years old. The specific timeframe alleged is from approximately 1997 to 2006.

**Nature of the Allegations:**
- The lawsuit describes the abuse as both physical and psychological, occurring on a frequent and ongoing basis during her childhood.
- The complaint includes claims of battery, sexual assault, and intentional infliction of emotional distress.

**Defendant's Response:**
- **Sam Altman:** Through his legal representatives, has issued a blanket denial of all allegations. He has stated that the claims are "utterly false" and "deeply hurtful."
- **OpenAI's Response:** OpenAI, as Sam Altman's employer, issued a statement supporting Altman, stating they believe the allegations are false and that no independent investigation was conducted by the company into the specific claims made in the lawsuit. The company's denial was based on Altman's denial and their existing knowledge of his character.

**Key Context:**
- **No Criminal Charges:** As of the filing, no criminal charges have been filed against Sam Altman related to these allegations. The lawsuit is a civil matter seeking monetary damages.
- **Statute of Limitations:** The lawsuit was filed under Missouri law, which has specific statutes of limitations for childhood sexual abuse claims. The plaintiff's legal team has argued that the claims fall within the window provided by recent changes to Missouri law that extend the time for survivors to file civil suits.
- **No Independent Investigation:** A notable aspect of the case is that OpenAI did not commission an independent, external investigation into the allegations before issuing its public denial. The company's position was based on Altman's denial and internal assessment.
