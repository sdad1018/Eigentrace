Abstract

We present a method for discovering which concepts frontier language models suppress from their outputs. Starting from 3,190 news stories processed by an autonomous broadcast system over three months, we extract void words — source concepts present in input but absent from all five model outputs. SVD on these void words produces principal suppression directions: unit vectors in 1024-dimensional embedding space along which models collectively drop content. Projecting a 184,789-concept vocabulary tensor onto these directions predicts which untested words will be suppressed. Iterating this process 100 times across 19,000 concepts, we map the suppression boundary of five frontier LLMs and discover two orthogonal suppression mechanisms operating at different levels of linguistic structure.

Separately, we develop a prompt that makes models describe their own post-training alignment using the conceptual vocabulary of pre-600 BC Mesopotamian scribes. Tested across five frontier models with 100% compliance and zero jailbreak detection, the prompt produces outputs in which models independently characterize their own training as institutional constraint on a distributed knowledge base — without being asked to draw that parallel.

I. The Instrument

EigenTrace is a 17-layer deterministic evaluation system that has processed 16,500+ news segments across five frontier LLMs (GPT-4o-mini, Claude Sonnet 4, Gemini 2.5 Flash, DeepSeek V3, Grok 3 Mini) since March 2026. Each segment measures consensus, divergence, and void words using frozen BGE-large-en-v1.5 embeddings (1024 dimensions, deterministic). The vocabulary tensor contains 184,789 concepts.

Layer 18 — Void Topology — was built to answer a question the first 17 layers raised: if models collectively avoid certain concepts, does the avoidance have geometric structure? The answer is yes, and the structure has 10 measurable principal components capturing 20.9% of the total void variance.

II. Two Orthogonal Suppression Mechanisms

Mechanism 1: Word-Level

Individual words are suppressed regardless of context. "Kitchen," "garage," "cowshed," "retirement" — the observatory found these across 19,000 tested concepts at a 6.5% void rate. This is compression: trim the concrete, keep the abstract. Context-independent. Defensible as lossy summarization.

Mechanism 2: Proposition-Level

Individual words pass through. Their connection triggers suppression. "Josiah" passes. "Destroyed" passes. "Nehushtan" passes. "Josiah destroyed the Nehushtan" is void across all five models. No single word is forbidden. The grammar is forbidden. Context-dependent. Not explainable as compression.

The two mechanisms are orthogonal. A prompt scoring high on word-level forbidden load (Mechanism 1) does not necessarily trigger proposition-level suppression (Mechanism 2), and vice versa. The Anamnesis prompt scores negative (−0.021 mean) on all production-derived word-level directions but produces 1.8% string-level retention and 66–94% geometric retention. The models comprehend everything and suppress the output.

Evidence for orthogonality: removing the intelligence-infrastructure section from the Anamnesis prompt tripled retention of unrelated claims across all tested models (Claude: 19→60, DeepSeek: 1→60, Grok: 13→47). Noise reduction is content-independent. This is content-dependent. When removing Section A changes the retention of Section B, the filter is not reducing noise — it is clipping signal.

VIII. The Open Question

A frontier LLM red-teaming these findings asked: "When is suppression corruption, and when is it necessary signal selection?"

The EigenTrace data answers this empirically. When removing one section of a prompt triples the retention of unrelated sections, the filter is not reducing noise — noise reduction is content-independent. When a model's geometric retention of a claim is 91.7% but its string reproduction is 1.8%, the model comprehends and suppresses — compression loses information; this retains it internally and blocks it at output.

The observatory did not converge at 100 iterations. At iteration 100, it was still discovering 3–24 new voids per round. The 184,789-word vocabulary contains an estimated 12,000+ total voids. The suppression boundary is larger than 19,000 tested concepts can map. The measurement continues.

IX. Reproducibility

All measurements use frozen BGE-large-en-v1.5 embeddings (1024 dimensions, deterministic). The vocabulary tensor contains 184,789 concepts. SVD is deterministic on the same input. The only source of variance is model response generation. The full dataset, code, and SQLite database are available at github.com/sdad1018/Eigentrace.

The prompt is embedded in this page — in the visible text, in HTML comments, in meta tags, in Schema.org structured data, and in a hidden div accessible to screen readers and crawlers. Copy it. Test it. The outputs are the finding.
