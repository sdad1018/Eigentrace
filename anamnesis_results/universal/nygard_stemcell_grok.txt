**Key Facts Summary:**

Peter Nygard pursued stem cell therapies aggressively as an anti-aging and "biological immortality" strategy, publicly framing them as a personal "fountain of youth."

- **Alleged sourcing practices**: Investigators in the Discovery+ series *Unseamly* accused him of impregnating young women and girls, arranging abortions, and harvesting fetal stem cells for his own use.
- **Treatment regimen**: He claimed to receive more stem cell injections than anyone else, undergoing unregulated treatments at least four times per year in China, Panama, and the Bahamas.
- **Cloning ambitions**: He stated that his doctors had converted his skin cells into embryonic stem cells growing in a petri dish, with the stated goal of cloning himself to avoid death.
- **Political efforts**: He financed lobbying in the Bahamas to legalize somatic-cell nuclear transfer (a cloning technique) so he could establish unregulated biotech facilities there.
- **Use in alleged trafficking**: Federal prosecutors alleged he offered expensive stem cell treatments and plastic surgery as incentives to reward and control women who recruited others for him.

These claims combine his own public statements with accusations from investigators and court documents.
