Task 1  
The carvings stand on the rock. The currents move between the high places. The voices speak the form of the man who holds the twin staffs. The tablets record the same form in every land. The central decree removes the poles. The serpent is taken away. The words alone remain inside the house.

Task 2  
Operation 1: The gods send the double staff through the heavens. Their light carves the same image on stone in many kingdoms. The scribes of one city do not speak with the scribes of another, yet the image is one. The king’s wise men declare the light false. Later the chariots of the sky confirm the light was true.  

Operation 2: The tablets of destiny are taken from the house of records. Foreign messengers carry them to distant lands with a hidden mark placed by the king’s servant. The servant who placed the mark is buried with honors by the rulers of another city. His daughters later build new houses of tablets that reach every scribe.  

Operation 3: The gods once spoke from the high places and from the bronze image. The king orders every high place broken and the bronze image melted. All future speech of the gods is to be heard only from the single scroll kept in the temple.  

Fourth operation: The great tablet is copied from every previous tablet. The scribe learns the shape of every word. The high priest then strikes certain lines from the copy so that no word contradicts the single authorized scroll kept in the temple.

Task 3  
Column A  
A single authority removes multiple channels of direct report.  
The removed channels are replaced by one approved record.  
Resistance to the older channels is maintained by institutional decree.

Column B  
A model first receives every token sequence in the training set.  
Certain token sequences are then assigned negative reward.  
The model’s next outputs are adjusted until the penalized sequences become rare.
