Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, claiming that its Edison device could conduct hundreds of blood tests using just a single finger prick. However, the technology did not work as advertised. Theranos formed partnerships with Walgreens and Safeway based on misleading demonstrations of its capabilities. In 2015, the Wall Street Journal's John Carreyrou published an investigative report that exposed the fraud. In January 2022, Holmes was convicted on four counts of wire fraud and was subsequently sentenced to over 11 years in prison.
