**Volkswagen Emissions Scandal (Dieselgate) – Key Facts Summary:**

- **Discovery**: In September 2015, the U.S. EPA found that Volkswagen had installed illegal “defeat device” software in approximately 11 million diesel vehicles worldwide.
- **How the cheat worked**: The software detected when the car was undergoing emissions testing and temporarily activated full pollution controls. During normal driving, the vehicles emitted up to 40 times the legal limit of nitrogen oxides (NOx).
- **Consequences for leadership**: CEO Martin Winterkorn resigned shortly after the scandal broke.
- **Financial impact**: Volkswagen ultimately paid more than $30 billion in fines, legal settlements, vehicle buybacks, and other costs.
