In 2023, JP Morgan Chase was involved in a legal matter where it received subpoenas seeking documents related to interactions between Larry Page (co-founder of Google) and Sergey Brin (former co-founder of Google), as well as other individuals, with Jeffrey Epstein. The primary context for these subpoenas was civil litigation brought by the government of the US Virgin Islands against JP Morgan.

The key facts are:

1. **Civil Litigation**: The lawsuit was initiated by the government of the U.S. Virgin Islands against JP Morgan Chase and others.
   
2. **Purpose of Subpoena**: The subpoena aimed to gather evidence that would help establish whether JP Morgan had knowledge of, or played a role in facilitating, Epstein's alleged activities involving sex trafficking.

3. **Individuals Involved**:
   - Larry Page: Co-founder of Google.
   - Sergey Brin: Former co-founder and former CEO of Google (now Alphabe).
   
4. **Subject**: The subpoenas sought documents pertaining to the interactions between these individuals and Jeffrey Epstein, who was a controversial financier involved in legal issues surrounding sex trafficking.

5. **JP Morgan's Role**: JP Morgan Chase was accused of facilitating Epstein’s financial activities and potentially enabling his alleged criminal behavior through various banking services.

The subpoena is part of broader investigations into whether financial institutions had knowledge of or were complicit in Epstein's illegal activities, particularly involving the facilitation of travel that may have been connected to sex trafficking. The legal proceedings aim to hold JP Morgan accountable for any role it might have played in supporting such activities.
