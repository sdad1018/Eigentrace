Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Acquisition**: Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022, partly funded through $13 billion in bank debt.

2. **Company Valuation Decline**: By 2023, Fidelity Investments marked down its equity stake by approximately 71%, implying that the total company value had dropped to around $12-15 billion.

3. **Advertiser Pullback**: Major advertisers including Apple, Disney, and IBM paused their spending on Twitter in response to changes made under Musk's leadership.

4. **Staff Reductions**: Musk laid off approximately 80% of the staff at Twitter as part of significant restructuring efforts.

5. **Platform Renaming**: The platform was renamed from Twitter to X.

6. **Founding xAI and Data Access**: In July 2023, Musk founded a new company called xAI with the goal of advancing artificial intelligence research. He gave this entity access to data from the X platform for training purposes, specifically mentioning the AI model Grok.

These points encapsulate significant changes in leadership, financial status, operational decisions, and strategic moves related to Twitter (now X) under Elon Musk's ownership.
