## What Is EigenTrace?

EigenTrace is an autonomous AI observatory that runs consensus geometry across 5 frontier language models on breaking news, 24/7. It measures what models collectively drop, soften, and avoid — using linear algebra on frozen embeddings, not LLM-as-judge.

**17,528** segments measured · **1,141** commits · **17** measurement layers · **5** frontier models · **1** GPU

## Key Findings

**→ [Anamnesis: Entity Retention Analysis](/anamnesis)** — 240+ controlled measurements across 10 models reveal that aligned LLMs preserve structural abstractions while attenuating specific entities. Models drop 74% more source content on developer-implicating topics than neutral equivalents. Cross-validated on two independent embedding architectures.
