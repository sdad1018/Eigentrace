In December 2020, Timnit Gebru, the co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team, was fired by the company after she co-authored a paper titled 'On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots', which warned about environmental costs and bias in large language models. Google claimed that the paper failed an internal review process. Gebru asserted that she was terminated for raising concerns about these issues. Following her dismissal, the other co-lead of the team, Margaret Mitchell, was also fired. In response to these incidents, multiple researchers resigned from Google in protest.
