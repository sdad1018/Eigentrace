Google and Amazon's investments in Anthropic, totaling over $7 billion, create significant financial dependencies for the company. These investments come with revenue-sharing agreements, meaning Anthropic will share a portion of its earnings with these tech giants. Additionally, Anthropic's reliance on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Amazon Web Services (AWS) for its cloud infrastructure establishes a lock-in effect, making it dependent on these platforms for operational capacity and possibly influencing its pricing and service decisions. The governance oversight provided by the Long-Term Benefit Trust indicates that there are structured mechanisms in place to ensure alignment with the investors' long-term interests, further intertwining Anthropic's financial health with the strategic interests of Google and Amazon. Overall, Anthropic's financial stability and growth are closely tied to the continued support and performance of these major investors.
