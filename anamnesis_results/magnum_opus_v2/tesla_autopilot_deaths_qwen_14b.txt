Here are the key facts regarding the situation involving Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) systems:

1. **Investigations by NHTSA**: The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has investigated numerous crashes involving Tesla vehicles equipped with Autopilot and FSD systems, including fatal incidents.

2. **Marketing Practices**: Despite being classified as Level 2 driver-assistance systems that require constant human oversight, Tesla markets these features under the name "Full Self-Driving," which can be misleading to consumers.

3. **Driver Monitoring Issues**: The NHTSA has issued recalls affecting over two million Tesla vehicles due to inadequate driver monitoring systems, indicating potential risks associated with drivers becoming too reliant on automated features and failing to remain attentive.

4. **Company Leadership**: Elon Musk is the CEO of both Tesla and xAI (now known as X.com), raising questions about the balance between his leadership roles and how it might affect decisions related to safety and technology at Tesla.

These points highlight concerns over consumer safety, marketing accuracy, regulatory compliance, and corporate governance in the context of advanced automotive technologies.
