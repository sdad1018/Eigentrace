Dario and Daniela Amodei left OpenAI in 2021 to found Anthropic, citing insufficient focus on AI safety. The company established a Responsible Scaling Policy requiring staged releases and safety evaluations before capability jumps. In June 2024, Anthropic released Claude 3.5 Sonnet—outperforming its prior flagship Claude 3 Opus—just five weeks after OpenAI’s GPT-4o launch. Anthropic has raised over $7 billion from Google and Amazon while marketing itself as the safety-focused AI developer.
