# The Brand's Long-Awaited Return: 2026 Honda Prelude Marks Historic Nameplate Revival

The 2026 Honda Prelude marks the long-awaited return of one of the brand's most iconic nameplates, reintroduced as a modern hybrid sport coupe that bridges Honda's engineering heritage with contemporary electrified performance technology. This revival shows what happens when a storied automaker commits to honoring its past while embracing future drivetrain innovation.

Honda has confirmed that the returning Prelude will launch with a two-motor hybrid system—a sophisticated drivetrain pairing responsive electric torque with a gasoline engine for balanced performance. Early previews show a low, wide stance, sleek body lines, and a fastback-inspired profile that honors the spirit of past Preludes while embracing modern Honda design language.

The brand's engineering priorities for this coupe are clearly defined. First comes hybrid performance with strong off-the-line response and improved efficiency—the hallmark of Honda's electrified approach. Second is driver-focused design with clean visibility and intuitive controls that reflect the automaker's commitment to the driving experience. Third is a sport-tuned chassis with a stiff body and precise suspension tuning, delivering the classic Honda coupe handling that enthusiasts expect.
