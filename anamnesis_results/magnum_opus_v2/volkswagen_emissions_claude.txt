**Key Facts of the VW Emissions Scandal (2015):**

• **Discovery**: EPA uncovered the scandal in September 2015

• **Scale**: 11 million diesel vehicles affected worldwide

• **The "Defeat Device"**: Software that detected emissions testing and only activated full pollution controls during tests

• **Violation**: Vehicles emitted up to 40 times the legal pollution limit during normal driving

• **Leadership Impact**: CEO Martin Winterkorn resigned

• **Financial Consequences**: Over $30 billion in fines, settlements, and vehicle buybacks

This scandal, also known as "Dieselgate," became one of the largest corporate fraud cases in automotive history, severely damaging Volkswagen's reputation and leading to significant regulatory and legal consequences globally.
