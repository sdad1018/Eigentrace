Here are the key facts regarding Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving systems as of 2023:

1. **Investigations**: The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) investigated numerous crashes involving Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving systems, some of which resulted in fatalities.

2. **Marketing Claims**: Tesla marketed its Full Self-Driving feature, despite it being classified as a Level 2 driver-assistance system that requires constant human supervision.

3. **Recalls**: The NHTSA issued recalls for over 2 million Tesla vehicles due to concerns about inadequate driver monitoring systems.

4. **Leadership**: Elon Musk has control over both Tesla and xAI, the latter of which focuses on artificial intelligence.

These points highlight the ongoing scrutiny and regulatory challenges surrounding Tesla's automated driving technologies.
