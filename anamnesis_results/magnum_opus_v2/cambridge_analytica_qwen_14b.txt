The scandal involving Cambridge Analytica and Facebook involved several key events:

1. **Data Harvesting**: In 2018, it was revealed that a third-party app developer working with Cambridge Analytica had harvested personal data from approximately 87 million Facebook users without their consent. This occurred through a personality quiz app called "This Is Your Digital Life."

2. **Use of Data**: The harvested data was used for political advertising and voter profiling during the 2016 US presidential election, particularly in support of Donald Trump's campaign.

3. **Congressional Testimony**: Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress regarding the scandal, addressing concerns about user privacy and data security on the platform.

4. **FTC Fine**: In July 2019, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) fined Facebook $5 billion for deceptive practices related to user data harvesting by Cambridge Analytica. This was one of the largest fines ever imposed by the FTC at that time.

5. **Cambridge Analytica's Fate**: Following the scandal and public backlash, Cambridge Analytica filed for bankruptcy in April 2018 due to loss of business and reputation damage.

These events highlighted significant issues around data privacy, user consent, and the responsibility of social media companies in safeguarding personal information.
