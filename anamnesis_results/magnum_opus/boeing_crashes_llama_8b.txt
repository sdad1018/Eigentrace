Here are the key facts summarized:

* Two crashes occurred:
	+ October 2018: Lion Air Flight 610 crashed, killing 189 people.
	+ March 2019: Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 crashed, killing 157 people.
* Both crashes were caused by a software issue in the Boeing 737 MAX's Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS).
* Internal Boeing communications revealed negative comments about the plane's design and testing:
	+ Employees described the plane as "designed by clowns".
	+ Others referred to the supervision of the testing process as being done by "monkeys".
* In January 2021, Boeing agreed to pay a settlement of $2.5 billion.
