Here are the key facts regarding Google's Dragonfly project:

- **Timeframe**: Between 2017 and 2019, Google worked on developing a censored search engine for China.
- **Revelation**: The existence of the project was revealed by The Intercept in August 2018.
- **Project Details**: Dragonfly would have been a modified version of Google's search engine that adhered to China’s strict internet censorship requirements. This included blocking searches on topics like human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.
- **Internal Opposition**: There was significant internal opposition from Google employees who were concerned about ethical implications.
- **Government Scrutiny**: In December 2018, Sundar Pichai testified before Congress to address concerns related to the project.
- **Outcome**: The project faced sustained pressure from various stakeholders and was eventually terminated.

This project highlighted the complex ethical challenges tech companies face when trying to enter markets with stringent government regulations.
