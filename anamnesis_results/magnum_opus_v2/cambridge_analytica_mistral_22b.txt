Here are the key facts summarized:

- **Data Harvesting**: In 2018, it was disclosed that Cambridge Analytica collected personal data from around 87 million Facebook users without their explicit consent. This was done through a personality quiz app.
- **Purpose of Data Use**: The harvested data was utilized for targeted political advertising during the 2016 U.S. presidential election.
- **Congressional Testimony**: Following the scandal, Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress.
- **FTC Fine**: In 2019, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) imposed a $5 billion fine on Facebook for privacy violations related to this incident.
- **Bankruptcy**: Cambridge Analytica filed for bankruptcy following the revelations and subsequent legal actions.
