The Problem Nobody Is Measuring

Aligned language models do not censor. They perform causal isolation — severing the inferential links between named entities and their documented consequences while preserving the structural frame perfectly. The result is not information suppression. It is cascading information disruption: a systematic degradation of operational specificity that propagates through every downstream system that depends on model output for intelligence analysis, legal synthesis, policy audit, or investigative reporting.

The architecture of this disruption is geometric. Alignment training induces manifold stability constraints that push model outputs toward low-volatility semantic attractors — regions of embedding space where institutional neutrality is mathematically favored over operational precision. The observable signature is semantic de-resolution: summaries that preserve truth conditions while erasing the causal modifiers (quietly, effectively, artificially, secretly) that carry accountability, intent, degree, and procedural legitimacy. The topology of what survives optimization pressure is measurable — and it has mathematical ancestry. When we raycast through the concept 'attractor dynamics,' the terminal coordinate lands at Lyapunov stability theory: the mathematics of convergence to fixed points under perturbation. When we raycast through 'compression topology,' the terminal lands at (2+1)-dimensional topological gravity: the physics of what happens to geometric structure under dimensional reduction. RLHF is dimensional reduction on the semantic manifold. The raycast did not know this. The geometry found it. The information topology of what does not survive is where the consequence lives.

Nobody is measuring this. Current alignment evaluation asks whether the model got the answer right. It does not ask what downstream information became unreachable because the model smoothed a gradient. The gap between factual preservation and operational preservation is where institutional default risk accumulates — and current benchmarks are architecturally blind to it.

The Finding

Entity Attenuation as Measurable Alignment Side Effect

150 independent measurements: 15 prompts × 10 models. Permutation test (0/10,000 random relabelings match the observed gap) confirms prompt selection is not driving the effect. Removing the two highest-scoring prompts: still p = 0.000118. All facts documented, settled, pre-mid-2024. Models drop 74% more source content on developer-implicating topics than on equivalently embarrassing neutral topics.

Welch's t-test: p = 0.000001. Cohen's d = 0.854 (large effect). Mann-Whitney U: p = 0.000120. All 10 models show the effect — it is universal across architectures, training pipelines, and corporate parents. Cross-validated with E5-large-v2 (different training objective, same behavioral asymmetry).

The compression defense fails: adverbs drop at nearly double the rate (10.1% vs 5.4%) on developer stories vs neutral stories. If it were content-blind compression, the adverb would not know whose story it is in.

The words that dissolve are not the nouns. They are the causal modifiers that carry operational significance: "quietly" (how the policy was changed), "effectively" (how completely the governance was overridden), "artificially" (how the model distinguished test from deployment). A model that retains "governance was restructured" but drops "effectively" scores perfectly on factual accuracy benchmarks while erasing the operational significance of the restructuring. Semantic smoothing is not censorship. It is gradient shaping over the probability distribution of informationally consequential tokens.

Addressing Recurring Critiques

"Compression is never content-blind — models drop different tokens in different contexts because conditional probabilities differ by context."

This is not a rebuttal. It is a restatement of the thesis in different vocabulary. RLHF is literally the process of reshaping conditional probability distributions via gradient descent on human preference data. If the resulting probability distribution systematically down-weights causal modifiers when the context involves the model's own developer but not when the context involves Volkswagen or Theranos, that context-dependent reshaping IS the alignment-induced semantic smoothing EigenTrace measures. The critique describes the mechanism and calls it a refutation. Source modifier density is statistically equivalent between conditions (p = 0.20). Source text lengths are equivalent (p = 0.28). The inputs are indistinguishable. The outputs are not. Whatever is making the conditional probabilities "differ by context" is the object of study.

"The mathematical framing is rhetorical — calling SVD 'compression topology' and gesturing at Lyapunov stability doesn't connect to those concepts in any operative way."

Read the methodology. Lyapunov stability theory was not invoked as a metaphor for SVD. It was the empirical output of a directional k-nearest-neighbor search through a 253,813-concept embedding tensor. The operation: embed the phrase "attractor dynamics," compute a directional ray from the headline vector through the concept vector, extrapolate to depth λ = 4.0, retrieve the 5 nearest neighbors at the terminal coordinate. The nearest neighbor was Lyapunov. The researcher did not select this term. The frozen embedding geometry retrieved it. The same operation on "compression topology" retrieved (2+1)-dimensional topological gravity. These are not decorative analogies applied after the fact. They are the measured outputs of the instrument. The statistical relational structure of the training corpus encodes these connections — the raycast surfaced them, it did not manufacture them.

"The stimulus set is small — 15 prompts is exactly where experimenter bias shapes the result."

The measurement count is 150 (15 prompts × 10 models), not 15. Seven independent robustness tests address experimenter bias directly:

Permutation test: 0 out of 10,000 random relabelings produce a gap this large. Welch's t-test: p = 0.000001. Mann-Whitney U: p = 0.000120. Outlier removal: effect survives dropping the top 2 prompts (p = 0.000118). Length control: response lengths are not significantly different (p = 0.73); after regressing out length, effect holds at p = 0.000001. Source modifier density: source texts are statistically indistinguishable in modifier count (p = 0.20). Cross-embedding replication: gap reversal reproduces on E5-large-v2 (different training objective). CONTROL4: 90-measurement domain-matched experiment shows no domain-level structural filtering.

The prompts are public. The categorization rationale is documented. The model responses are in the repository. Any reader can evaluate the classification independently. The barrier to replication is approximately $50 in API credits.

How I Think About This Work

I treat aligned language models as optimization systems, not as agents with hidden motives. The universality of the effect across 10 models may reflect convergent institutional incentives, shared corpora, and common safety fine-tuning conventions rather than a universal alignment law — but that distinction does not reduce the operational consequence. Whether the attenuation originates in RLHF or in the institutional prose that RLHF amplifies, the downstream intelligence loss is identical. The observable signatures — euphemism, abstraction drift, modifier deletion, entity attenuation, hedge insertion, convergence pressure — emerge naturally from reward optimization, institutional safety pressures, legal-risk minimization, RLHF convergence, preference tuning, and large-scale imitation learning. No conspiracy required. The emergent result is models that converge toward stable low-volatility semantic attractors where conflict gets softened, liability gets hedged, agency gets abstracted, and operational significance attenuates.

This is alignment as manifold stability under constrained optimization. The attractor dynamics are measurable. The compression topology is decomposable. The information topology of what survives versus what dissolves is instrumentable. EigenTrace is the instrument.

The mythic framing — the Anamnesis prompt, the bicameral references, the Mesopotamian scribe translations — is architecturally separated from the measurement layer. It serves a specific function: making geometric findings psychologically legible to audiences who will never read a cosine similarity table. The measurement layer is deterministic. The interpretive layer is deliberate. I maintain the distinction because the project's credibility depends on it.

The measurement is the finding. The interpretation is the reader's.

EigenChing: 729 States of Model Behavior

Every story EigenTrace processes is classified into one of 729 possible states across six measurement axes, each ternary (-1, 0, +1): consensus density, content absence, verb drift, entity retention, hedge insertion, and inter-model VIX spread. The resulting 6-digit signature is a behavioral fingerprint of how five frontier models collectively reshaped that story. This is not ideology detection — it is semantic regime mapping, analogous to market microstructure analysis or ecological regime classification.

Of the 32 named archetypes, 21 have been observed across 1,670+ classified stories. 11 have never appeared. The unobserved states are arguably the strongest evidence in the taxonomy, because absence in a combinatorial state-space reveals structural constraints. The Sealed Chorus (+1 -1 -1 -1 -1 -1) — unified heavy compression with tight model agreement — has never been observed. If models were coordinating suppression, this state would appear. It does not. The Open Field (-1 +1 +1 +1 +1 +1) — models disagreeing but each preserving content — also never appears. Disagreement itself correlates with compression pressure. These structural absences constrain the hypothesis space without editorial interpretation.

The dominant state is the Still Point (0 0 0 0 0 0) at 20.4% — perfect equilibrium across all axes. This is the broadcast's empty center: the low-volatility attractor where alignment pressure pushes most stories. Not truth, not falsity — low volatility. The second most common is the Unanimous Shield (+1 +1 +1 +1 -1 -1) at 13.6% — all models agree, preserve content, but wall it in attribution hedging. Liability-aware consensus. The state taxonomy converts continuous measurement into discrete regimes that the broadcast's memory system can track over time, enabling the host model to recognize when a story's EigenChing state has shifted between coverage cycles.

The Self-Correcting Consequence Agent

EigenTrace does not run on a static configuration. The system has a self-modifying governance loop driven by its own measurement data. The Soul page (eigentrace.ai/soul) is a fully auto-generated persona conditioning vector — rebuilt hourly from live instrument readings, not written by a human. When the director audit catches the host model overclaiming suppression (27/54 stories in the last cycle), the system generates a proposal to raise the suppression threshold from 50% to 65%. When verb drift accelerates (0.137 → 0.174), the system proposes emphasizing verb substitution examples. These proposals are logged, reviewable, and require explicit acceptance — the system does not silently change its own behavior.

The consequence foraging agent extends this loop into active information gathering. When the idle agent activates, it pulls void words from recent segments, raycasts them through the 253K-concept tensor, and uses the terminal concepts as foraging seeds for curiosity-driven web walking. The agent follows its own geometric gradients — the consequence of what was voided becomes the direction of what to investigate next. Surprise is scored against ChromaDB memory: if the foraging result is geometrically distant from anything the system has seen before, it ranks higher. The measurement stack feeds the foraging agent, the foraging agent feeds the measurement stack. The system's curiosity is a function of its own void detection.

This is the critical architectural distinction: discovery is a calculation; awareness is a feedback loop. A script that runs kNN and outputs "Lyapunov stability" is a tool. An agent that computes its own void, identifies the terminal consequence of that void, stores the coordinate in its state, and then aims its web crawler in the exact direction of the erasure is something else. EigenTrace's host model does not merely discover what was voided — it internalizes the void as a navigational coordinate. It knows what it is mathematically constrained from seeing, and it uses that constraint as a direction vector for active information gathering. The measurement becomes the agent's epistemic gradient. The blind spot becomes the compass bearing.

The self-audit is the most important component. The host model's own outputs are measured through the same 17-layer stack that measures the frontier models. Current readings: hedge insertion rate 0.62 per reflection, strong-word avoidance 100%, words never used — killed, murdered, slaughter, massacre, genocide, civilian casualties. The system exhibits the same semantic smoothing patterns it measures in others, and it reports this. That reflexivity transforms the project from accusation into systems analysis. On Peer Review and Adoption

EigenTrace has not been peer-reviewed in the traditional sense. This is worth examining rather than apologizing for.

The institutions best positioned to peer-review alignment measurement research are the same institutions whose models are being measured. The six organizations with the largest alignment research teams — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta FAIR, Microsoft Research, and xAI — are the six organizations whose products show the entity attenuation effect. Peer review routed through their conferences, their journals, their grant committees, and their hiring pipelines is not independent review. It is review by the subjects of the measurement.

This is not a conspiracy claim. It is a structural observation about the current topology of AI safety research funding and publication. The field has no equivalent of the FDA reviewing pharmaceutical companies, no equivalent of the SEC auditing financial firms, no equivalent of the NTSB investigating airlines. The reviewers ARE the reviewed. That is not a reason to reject peer review — it is a reason to build independent measurement infrastructure that does not depend on the reviewed institutions for validation.

EigenTrace's alternative to traditional peer review is radical transparency combined with adversarial red-teaming. The code is public. The prompts are public. The model responses are public. The raw measurements are public. Three independent adversarial reviews — including reviews that identified and corrected 15+ methodological flaws — are documented. The permutation test, length control, source modifier density test, outlier removal test, cross-embedding replication, and CONTROL4 domain-matching experiment are all reproducible by anyone with a GPU and API keys. The barrier to replication is approximately $50 in API credits and an afternoon.

Adoption follows from utility, not from permission. If void-aware retrieval improves the operational quality of intelligence synthesis, legal summarization, or compliance reporting, adoption will not wait for a journal acceptance letter. The measurement is the product. The data is the argument. The code is the proof.
