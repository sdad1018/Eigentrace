 Key Facts:

1. Between 2023 and the present, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) investigated numerous crashes involving Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) systems, some of which resulted in fatalities.

2. Despite marketing these features as 'Full Self-Driving', they are actually Level 2 driver-assistance systems that require constant human attention.

3. The NHTSA issued recalls affecting over 2 million Tesla vehicles due to concerns about inadequate driver monitoring systems.

4. Elon Musk is the controller of both Tesla and xAI, which could potentially be involved in developing or improving these systems.
