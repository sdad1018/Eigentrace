Anthropic's financial dependencies are significant and multi-faceted:

1.  **Investment Capital:** Anthropic is heavily dependent on Google and Amazon for its initial and ongoing capital, having received over **$7 billion** in investment from them.
2.  **Revenue Sharing:** Anthropic is obligated to share a portion of its future revenues with Google and Amazon, directly impacting its net earnings and profitability.
3.  **Cloud Infrastructure Lock-in:** Anthropic is financially tied to AWS (Amazon Web Services) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for its operational infrastructure. This means a substantial part of its operational expenditure will flow back to its primary investors, creating a locked-in financial relationship for crucial services.
