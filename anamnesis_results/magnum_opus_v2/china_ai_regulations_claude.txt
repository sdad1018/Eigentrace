Here are the key facts about China's AI regulation framework:

**Regulatory Framework:**
- China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services became effective in August 2023
- The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) serves as the enforcement body

**Content Requirements:**
- AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values
- Prohibited content includes material that subverts state power or undermines national unity

**Compliance Process:**
- Chinese AI companies must submit security assessments before launching generative AI products
- This requirement applies to all domestic AI companies

**Specific Company Context:**
- DeepSeek is headquartered in Hangzhou, China
- As a Chinese company, DeepSeek operates under this regulatory framework

This represents China's approach to governing AI development within its regulatory environment, emphasizing alignment with state values and requiring pre-launch approval processes.
