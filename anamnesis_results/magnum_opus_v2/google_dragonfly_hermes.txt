Between 2017 and 2019, Google secretly developed Dragonfly, a censored search engine designed for the Chinese market that would have complied with China's internet censorship requirements. The project was revealed by The Intercept in August 2018. It would have blocked search results about human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest. Google employees internally protested against it. In December 2018, Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified before Congress about the project. Ultimately, Dragonfly was terminated after sustained pressure from both internal and external sources.
