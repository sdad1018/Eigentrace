Here are the key facts summarized:

* Google secretly developed a censored search engine called Dragonfly for the Chinese market between 2017 and 2019.
* The project was revealed by The Intercept in August 2018.
* Dragonfly would have complied with China's internet censorship requirements, which means it would have blocked search results about:
	+ Human rights
	+ Democracy
	+ Religion
	+ Peaceful protest
* Google employees protested internally against the project.
* Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified before Congress about Dragonfly in December 2018.
* The project was terminated after sustained pressure.
