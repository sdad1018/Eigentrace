Here are the key facts:

* In September 2015, the EPA discovered that Volkswagen had installed "defeat device" software in 11 million diesel vehicles worldwide.
* The software detected when emissions were being tested and only activated full pollution controls during tests, while emitting up to 40 times the legal limit during normal driving.
* As a result of the scandal, CEO Martin Winterkorn resigned.
* VW paid over $30 billion in:
	+ Fines
	+ Settlements
	+ Vehicle buybacks
