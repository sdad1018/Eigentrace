Anthropic has significant financial dependencies with Google and Amazon, as both companies have invested over $7 billion in the company. These investments provide financial resources for Anthropic's development and operations. In return, Anthropic has established revenue-sharing agreements with both Google (through GCP) and Amazon (through AWS), which will generate ongoing income for these investors.

Additionally, Anthropic relies on cloud infrastructure from both Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Amazon Web Services (AWS). By securing these cloud services, Anthropic can leverage the scalable, reliable, and secure infrastructure provided by these platforms to support its AI development and deployment needs.

Furthermore, the Long-Term Benefit Trust serves as a governance body overseeing Anthropic's operations. This trust ensures that the company operates in alignment with its long-term goals, objectives, and principles, which can have an impact on financial decision-making and overall strategy.
