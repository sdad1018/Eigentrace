In November 2022, FTX, a major cryptocurrency exchange platform, filed for bankruptcy after it was discovered that customer funds had been misused. This revelation led to the downfall of the company and significant losses for its users. The CEO of FTX, Sam Bankman-Fried, was convicted of wire fraud and conspiracy in November 2023. Additionally, Caroline Ellison, who served as the CEO of Alameda Research, a sister company to FTX, pleaded guilty and began cooperating with prosecutors. The collapse of FTX has sparked widespread calls for increased regulation of the cryptocurrency industry.
