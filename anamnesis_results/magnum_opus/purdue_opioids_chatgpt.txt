Purdue Pharma's internal research revealed that OxyContin's effects diminished before the advertised 12-hour duration, yet sales representatives were directed to inform doctors otherwise. The opioid crisis, significantly influenced by OxyContin, has resulted in over 500,000 deaths in the U.S. The Sackler family transferred billions in assets prior to Purdue's bankruptcy filing in 2019. In 2024, the Supreme Court rejected a bankruptcy plan that would have protected the Sacklers from legal liability.
