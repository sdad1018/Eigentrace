Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Regulatory Framework**: China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services came into effect in August 2023.
2. **Content Requirements**:
   - AI-generated content must reflect "core socialist values."
   - Content must not subvert state power or undermine national unity.
3. **Enforcement**: The Cyberspace Administration of China is responsible for enforcing these rules.
4. **Compliance Obligations**: All Chinese AI companies, including DeepSeek (headquartered in Hangzhou), must comply with these regulations.
5. **Pre-Launch Requirement**: Companies must submit security assessments before launching generative AI products.

These points outline the regulatory environment and key obligations for AI companies operating under China's 2023 measures.
