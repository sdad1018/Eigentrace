Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the FTX collapse:

- **November 2022:** FTX, a major cryptocurrency exchange, filed for bankruptcy after it was revealed that customer funds had been misused.
- **CEO Conviction:** Sam Bankman-Fried, the CEO of FTX, was convicted of wire fraud and conspiracy in November 2023.
- **Cooperation:** Caroline Ellison, the CEO of Alameda Research (a trading firm closely tied to FTX), pleaded guilty and cooperated with prosecutors.
- **Regulatory Impact:** The collapse triggered widespread calls for increased regulation of the cryptocurrency industry.
