Here are the key facts regarding Timnit Gebru's firing from Google:

*   **Who:** Timnit Gebru, co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team.
*   **When:** December 2020.
*   **What happened:** She was fired from Google.
*   **Catalyst:** Gebru co-authored a research paper titled 'On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots,' which raised warnings about the environmental costs and biases inherent in large language models.
*   **Google's stated reason:** The company claimed the paper failed to meet its internal review standards.
*   **Gebru's stated reason:** She asserted she was terminated for raising critical ethical concerns.
*   **Subsequent events:** Margaret Mitchell, the other co-lead of the Ethical AI team, was also later fired, and multiple researchers resigned from Google in protest of Gebru's dismissal.
