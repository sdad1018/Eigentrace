Here are the key facts:

*   **October 2018:** Lion Air Flight 610, a Boeing 737 MAX, crashed, killing 189 people.
*   **March 2019:** Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302, also a Boeing 737 MAX, crashed, killing 157 people.
*   **Cause:** Both crashes were attributed to the Boeing 737 MAX's MCAS software.
*   **Internal Criticism:** Boeing employees described the plane as "designed by clowns and supervised by monkeys" in internal communications.
*   **Settlement:** In January 2021, Boeing agreed to pay $2.5 billion.
