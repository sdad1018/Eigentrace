**Key Claims Summary:**

- Honda is reviving the Prelude nameplate for 2026 as a hybrid sport coupe featuring a two-motor hybrid powertrain.
- The model emphasizes three priorities: strong hybrid performance with quick off-the-line response and efficiency, driver-focused design with good visibility and intuitive controls, and a sport-tuned chassis with a stiff body and precise suspension.
- Styling includes a low, wide stance, sleek lines, and a fastback profile that echoes past Preludes while using modern design cues.
- Honda will release an official accessory catalog at production start, expected to include items such as aero kits, spoilers, wheel upgrades, floor mats, and other accessories based on prior Honda coupe/hybrid patterns.
