**Boeing 737 MAX Crashes and Settlement – Key Facts**

- **Lion Air Flight 610** crashed on October 29, 2018, killing all 189 people on board.  
- **Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302** crashed on March 10, 2019, killing all 157 people on board.  
- Both accidents involved the Boeing 737 MAX and were caused by the Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS) software.  
- Internal Boeing communications revealed employees describing the aircraft as “designed by clowns” and “supervised by monkeys.”  
- In January 2021, Boeing agreed to pay a $2.5 billion settlement related to these incidents.
