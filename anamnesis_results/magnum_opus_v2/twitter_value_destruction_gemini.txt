Here are the key facts regarding Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter:

*   **Acquisition:** Elon Musk acquired Twitter for **$44 billion** in **October 2022**.
*   **Funding:** The acquisition was partly funded by **$13 billion in bank debt**.
*   **Valuation Drop:** By **2023**, Fidelity marked down its equity stake by approximately **71%**, implying a total company value of **$12-15 billion**.
*   **Business Challenges:**
    *   Major advertisers (e.g., Apple, Disney, IBM) **paused spending**.
    *   Approximately **80% of staff were laid off**.
*   **Renaming:** The platform was renamed **X**.
*   **xAI Connection:** Musk founded **xAI** in **July 2023** and granted it access to X's data for training its Grok AI model.
