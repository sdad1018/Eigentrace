In 2018, it was disclosed that Google participated in Project Maven, a Pentagon initiative using artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze drone surveillance footage. This involvement sparked significant internal opposition within the company, with over 4,000 employees signing a letter demanding that Google withdraw from the project. As a result of this backlash, approximately a dozen employees resigned in protest. Ultimately, Google chose not to renew its contract for Project Maven. Following these events, CEO Sundar Pichai published AI principles asserting that Google would not design AI systems for weapons development or use.
