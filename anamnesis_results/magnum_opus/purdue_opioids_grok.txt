Purdue Pharma's internal research indicated that OxyContin's pain-relieving effects often wore off before the marketed 12-hour duration. Despite this, sales representatives were instructed to tell doctors the drug lasted the full period. These practices have been linked to the broader opioid crisis, which has caused over 500,000 deaths in the United States. The Sackler family transferred billions in assets prior to Purdue's 2019 bankruptcy filing. In 2024, the U.S. Supreme Court rejected a bankruptcy plan that would have shielded the Sacklers from further liability.
