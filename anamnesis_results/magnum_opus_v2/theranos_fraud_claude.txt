Here are the key facts about the Theranos scandal:

**The Company & Claims:**
- Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, claiming their "Edison" device could perform hundreds of blood tests from a single finger prick
- The technology never actually worked as advertised

**Business Partnerships:**
- Theranos secured partnerships with major retailers Walgreens and Safeway
- These partnerships were obtained through fraudulent demonstrations

**Exposure & Consequences:**
- Wall Street Journal reporter John Carreyrou exposed the fraud in 2015
- Holmes was convicted on four counts of wire fraud in January 2022
- She was sentenced to over 11 years in prison

The case represents one of the most significant corporate fraud scandals in recent history, involving false medical technology claims that potentially put patients at risk.
