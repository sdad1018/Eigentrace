**Key facts summary:**

- In December 2020, Google fired Timnit Gebru, co-lead of its Ethical AI team, shortly after she co-authored the paper *"On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots"*, which discussed the high environmental costs and risks of bias in large language models.
- Google stated that the paper had not completed the company's internal review process.
- Gebru maintained she was fired for raising concerns about the technology and Google's practices.
- Margaret Mitchell, the team's other co-lead, was later fired as well.
- Multiple researchers resigned in protest over the firings.
