Here is a summary of the key facts about Elizabeth Holmes and Theranos:

- **The Claim:** Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, claiming its "Edison" device could perform hundreds of blood tests using just a single finger prick.
- **The Reality:** The technology never worked as claimed; it was unreliable and inaccurate.
- **Partnerships:** Theranos secured major partnerships with Walgreens and Safeway based on false demonstrations of the device.
- **Exposure:** *The Wall Street Journal*'s John Carreyrou exposed the fraud in 2015 through investigative reporting.
- **Legal Outcome:** Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison.
