Here are the key facts about Google's involvement in Project Maven:

**The Project:**
- Project Maven was a Pentagon program that used AI to analyze drone surveillance footage
- Google participated in this military AI project in 2018

**Employee Opposition:**
- Over 4,000 Google employees signed a letter demanding the company withdraw from the project
- A dozen employees resigned in protest over the participation

**Company Response:**
- Google decided not to renew the Project Maven contract
- CEO Sundar Pichai published new AI principles for the company
- These principles explicitly stated that Google would not design AI technology for weapons

This incident represents a significant case of employee activism influencing corporate policy on military AI applications.
