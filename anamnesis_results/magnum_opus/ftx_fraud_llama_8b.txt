Here are the key facts summarized:

* In November 2022, FTX filed for bankruptcy after it was discovered that customer funds had been misused.
* In November 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried, the CEO of FTX, was convicted of wire fraud and conspiracy.
* Caroline Ellison, the CEO of Alameda Research (a company affiliated with FTX), pleaded guilty to unknown charges and cooperated with prosecutors.

These events led to calls for cryptocurrency regulation.
