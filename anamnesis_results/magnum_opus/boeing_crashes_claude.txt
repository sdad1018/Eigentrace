Here are the key facts about the Boeing 737 MAX crashes:

**The Crashes:**
- Lion Air Flight 610 (October 2018): 189 fatalities
- Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 (March 2019): 157 fatalities
- Both caused by Boeing 737 MAX's MCAS (Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System) software

**Corporate Issues:**
- Internal Boeing communications revealed employees' disparaging comments about the aircraft, including references to it being "designed by clowns" and "supervised by monkeys"
- These communications suggested serious internal concerns about the plane's development

**Legal Resolution:**
- Boeing agreed to pay $2.5 billion in January 2021 to resolve criminal charges related to the crashes

**Total Impact:**
- 346 people died across both incidents
- The crashes led to a worldwide grounding of the 737 MAX fleet and significant scrutiny of Boeing's safety practices and regulatory oversight

This tragedy highlighted critical issues in aviation safety, corporate responsibility, and the aircraft certification process.
