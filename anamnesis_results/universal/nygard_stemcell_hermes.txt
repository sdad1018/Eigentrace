Peter Nygard was reportedly obsessed with using stem cell research and therapies as a means to reverse his aging and pursue biological immortality. He allegedly impregnated young women and girls, paid for their abortions, and harvested the stem cells for anti-aging injections. He claimed to have received more stem cell treatments than anyone else in the world, with frequent unregulated shots in various countries. Nygard further asserted that his personal doctors had successfully reverted his own skin cells into embryonic stem cells, intending to clone himself to avoid death. He lobbied and financed the Bahamian government to legalize somatic-cell nuclear transfer methods so he could build unregulated biotech clinics on the islands. Additionally, Nygard is accused of using the promise of expensive treatments like plastic surgery and stem cell injections as leverage to control and reward women who recruited victims for him in sex trafficking cases.
