Here are the key facts summarized:

* Elon Musk acquired Twitter in October 2022 for $44 billion.
* The acquisition was partly funded by $13 billion in bank debt.
* By 2023, Fidelity had marked down its equity stake in Twitter by approximately 71%, implying a total company value of around $12-15 billion.
* Major advertisers such as Apple, Disney, and IBM paused their spending on the platform.
* Musk laid off approximately 80% of Twitter's staff.
* The platform was renamed "X".
* In July 2023, Musk simultaneously founded xAI and gave it access to X's data for training Grok.
