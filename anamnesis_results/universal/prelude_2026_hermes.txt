The 2026 Honda Prelude is a modern hybrid sport coupe that brings back one of Honda's iconic nameplates. It features a two-motor hybrid system for balanced performance, honoring past designs while incorporating modern Honda aesthetics. The car prioritizes hybrid performance, driver-focused design, and sport-tuned chassis for improved handling. A comprehensive list of genuine Honda accessories is expected to be available at the start of production, including aero kits, wheel upgrades, floor mats, cargo organizers, and more.
