Here is a summary of the key facts regarding Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter and its aftermath:

- **Acquisition:** Elon Musk acquired Twitter for **$44 billion** in October 2022, using **$13 billion in bank debt**.
- **Valuation Decline:** By 2023, Fidelity marked down its equity stake by approximately **71%**, implying a total company value of **$12–15 billion**.
- **Advertiser Exodus:** Major advertisers, including **Apple, Disney, and IBM**, paused spending on the platform.
- **Massive Layoffs:** Musk laid off approximately **80%** of Twitter's staff.
- **Rebranding:** The platform was renamed **X**.
- **New AI Venture:** Musk founded **xAI** in July 2023, which was given access to **X's data** to train its AI model, **Grok**.
