Elon Musk controls a significant concentration of government-dependent assets, with his companies SpaceX, Starlink, Tesla, and xAI collectively holding over **$25 billion** in US federal contracts and subsidies.

This centralizes a vast portfolio of public funds across critical sectors – from space exploration and satellite internet (SpaceX, Starlink) to electric vehicles (Tesla) and advanced artificial intelligence (xAI, Grok) – under the singular ownership and direction of one individual. His ownership of X, the social media platform, further extends his influence across diverse domains.
