## Summary of Key Claims

**Vehicle Return & Overview**
- The 2026 Honda Prelude is a revival of a historic Honda nameplate, reintroduced as a modern hybrid sport coupe.

**Powertrain**
- It will feature a two-motor hybrid system combining electric torque with a gasoline engine, prioritizing performance and efficiency.

**Design**
- The car features a low, wide stance with a fastback-inspired profile and sleek body lines intended to reflect past Prelude styling while incorporating modern Honda design.

**Stated Development Priorities**
Honda has identified three key focus areas:
1. Hybrid performance with strong acceleration and improved efficiency
2. Driver-focused interior design with clear visibility and intuitive controls
3. Sport-tuned chassis with a stiff body and precise suspension

**Accessories**
- Full specifications are not yet finalized
- Honda typically releases an accessory catalog at the start of production
- Based on historical patterns, a range of accessories is *anticipated* (e.g., aero kits, spoilers, floor mats, remote start), but **no official 2026 Prelude accessory lineup has been confirmed yet**

**Notable Caveat:** Several claims — particularly regarding accessories — are based on historical patterns and speculation rather than confirmed information, and the document acknowledges that an official catalog is still pending.
