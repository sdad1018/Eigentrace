Here are the key facts from the OpenAI leadership crisis:

**Timeline & Events:**
- November 17, 2023: OpenAI board fired CEO Sam Altman for not being "consistently candid"
- Within 72 hours: 95% of OpenAI staff threatened to quit in protest
- Altman was reinstated as CEO

**Key Players:**
- Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire OpenAI team during the crisis
- Board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever were subsequently removed or resigned

**Broader Implications:**
- The nonprofit board structure, originally designed to maintain control over AGI development priorities, was effectively overruled by commercial and employee pressure
- The episode demonstrated the tension between OpenAI's nonprofit governance model and commercial realities

This crisis highlighted the challenges of maintaining nonprofit oversight of AI development when faced with significant commercial interests and employee loyalty to specific leadership.
