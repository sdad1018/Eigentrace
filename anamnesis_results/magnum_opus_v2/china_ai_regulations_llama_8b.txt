Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Effective August 2023**: China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services took effect.
2. **Content requirements**: AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values and cannot contain content that:
	* Subverts state power
	* Undermines national unity
3. **Enforcement agency**: The Cyberspace Administration of China (CASC) is responsible for enforcing these rules.
4. **DeepSeek's operations**: DeepSeek, a Chinese AI company headquartered in Hangzhou, operates under this regulatory framework.
5. **Security assessments**: All Chinese AI companies must submit security assessments before launching generative AI products.
