Here are the key facts about Google's Project Dragonfly:

**What**: A censored search engine designed for China that would block results on human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest

**When**: Secretly developed 2017-2019, revealed in August 2018, terminated after sustained pressure

**Key Events**:
- The Intercept exposed the project in August 2018
- Google employees protested internally against the project
- CEO Sundar Pichai testified before Congress about it in December 2018
- Project was ultimately cancelled due to continued pressure

**Significance**: The controversy highlighted tensions between tech companies' business interests and human rights concerns when operating in authoritarian markets.
