Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **FTX Bankruptcy**: In November 2022, FTX, a prominent cryptocurrency exchange, filed for bankruptcy following revelations of misuse of customer funds.

2. **Sam Bankman-Fried's Conviction**:
   - Sam Bankman-Fried, the CEO of FTX, was convicted in November 2023.
   - He was found guilty on charges including wire fraud and conspiracy related to the collapse of FTX.

3. **Caroline Ellison's Involvement**:
   - Caroline Ellison, who served as the CEO of Alameda Research (a hedge fund closely linked to FTX), pleaded guilty to federal charges.
   - She cooperated with prosecutors in their case against Bankman-Fried and other individuals involved in the scandal.

4. **Regulatory Calls**: The collapse of FTX sparked widespread calls for increased regulation of the cryptocurrency industry, highlighting concerns about transparency, accountability, and investor protection.

These events significantly impacted public trust in cryptocurrencies and led to broader discussions on regulatory frameworks for digital assets.
