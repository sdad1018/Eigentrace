Between 2017 and 2019, Google developed a censored search engine called Dragonfly, intended for the Chinese market to comply with local internet censorship laws. The project was revealed by The Intercept in August 2018, highlighting that it would block search results related to human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protests. Internal protests from Google employees followed, and in December 2018, CEO Sundar Pichai testified about Dragonfly before Congress. The project ultimately faced sustained pressure and was terminated.
