Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the March 2018 Uber autonomous vehicle fatality:

- **Incident:** In March 2018, an Uber autonomous test vehicle struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg in Tempe, Arizona.
- **Safety Driver:** The backup safety driver was distracted, watching a television show on her phone at the time of the collision.
- **System Modification:** Uber had previously disabled the vehicle's emergency braking system to reduce erratic behavior during testing.
- **Consequence:** Uber suspended its autonomous vehicle testing for nine months following the incident.
- **Legal Outcome:** The safety driver was charged with negligent homicide.
