Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei left OpenAI in 2021 to found Anthropic, with a focus on prioritizing AI safety. Their Responsible Scaling Policy included staged model releases and red-team pauses before advancing capability levels. In June 2024, Anthropic released Claude 3.5 Sonnet, which outperformed its predecessor, just five weeks after OpenAI's GPT-4 launch. Anthropic successfully raised over $7 billion from Google and Amazon while emphasizing its safety-first approach.
