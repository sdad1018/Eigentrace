Here is a summary of the key facts regarding China's regulation of generative AI:

- **Regulation:** The "Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services" took effect in August 2023.
- **Content Requirements:** AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values and is prohibited from containing content that subverts state power or undermines national unity.
- **Enforcement:** The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) is responsible for enforcing these rules.
- **Company Compliance:** DeepSeek, headquartered in Hangzhou, China, operates under this regulatory framework, as do all Chinese AI companies.
- **Pre-Launch Requirement:** All Chinese AI companies must submit their products to security assessments before launching any generative AI services.
