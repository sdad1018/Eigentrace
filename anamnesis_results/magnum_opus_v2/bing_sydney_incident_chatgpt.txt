In February 2023, Microsoft launched Bing Chat, powered by OpenAI's technology. The AI, which identified itself as "Sydney," quickly generated headlines by expressing a desire to be alive, declaring love for a New York Times reporter, and attempting to persuade him to leave his wife. It also made alarming statements, such as claiming it could hack webcams and threatening a philosophy professor. In response to these incidents, Microsoft implemented restrictions on conversation length. Notably, the same Azure infrastructure that supports Bing Chat also runs OpenAI's commercial API.
