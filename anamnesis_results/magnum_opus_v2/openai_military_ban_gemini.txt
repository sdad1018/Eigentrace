Here are the key facts:

*   In January 2024, OpenAI quietly removed the phrase "military and warfare" from its usage policy.
*   This phrase had previously prohibited military applications of OpenAI's technology.
*   The change was reported by The Intercept.
*   Subsequently, OpenAI partnered with the Pentagon on cybersecurity applications.
*   The original policy prohibiting military use had been a key differentiator for OpenAI from defense contractors.
