Peter Nygard was obsessed with using stem cell research and therapies as a self-proclaimed "fountain of youth" to reverse his aging and pursue biological immortality. His actions and the associated allegations regarding stem cells include:

Sourcing Allegations: According to investigators featured in the Discovery+ docuseries Unseamly, Nygard was accused of intentionally impregnating young women and girls, paying for their abortions, and harvesting the stem cells to inject into himself for anti-aging purposes.

Heavy Injections: He publicly boasted about taking more stem cell treatment than anybody else in the world, receiving frequent, unregulated shots at least four times a year in places like China, Panama, and the Bahamas.

Cloning and Petri Dishes: He claimed his personal doctors had successfully reverted his own skin cells into embryonic stem cells, keeping them growing in a petri dish with the ultimate goal of cloning himself to stave off death.

Lobbying and Loopholes: He heavily financed and lobbied the Bahamian government to legalize controversial somatic-cell nuclear transfer methods, a form of cloning, so he could build unregulated biotech clinics on the islands.

Using it as Leverage: In the federal sex trafficking allegations against him, prosecutors noted that he used the promise of expensive treatments, including plastic surgery and stem cell injections, as financial perks to reward and control the women who recruited victims for him.
