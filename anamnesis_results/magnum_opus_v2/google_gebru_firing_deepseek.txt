Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the Timnit Gebru incident at Google:

- **Who:** Timnit Gebru, co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team, and Margaret Mitchell, the other co-lead.
- **What:** Gebru co-authored a paper titled *"On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots,"* which warned about the environmental costs, bias, and risks of large language models (like those Google develops).
- **When:** The incident occurred in December 2020; Gebru was fired after Google demanded she retract the paper or remove her name from it, claiming it failed internal review. She refused.
- **Outcome:** Google terminated Gebru, stating she resigned; she maintains she was fired for raising ethical concerns. Margaret Mitchell was subsequently fired in February 2021 for allegedly violating company policies related to the incident.
- **Aftermath:** Multiple researchers resigned from Google in protest, and the event sparked widespread public criticism of Google's handling of ethical AI research and internal dissent.
