Here are the key facts summarized:

- **Founder and Company**: Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, a healthcare technology company.
- **Claimed Technology**: Theranos claimed its proprietary device, called Edison, could run hundreds of blood tests from a single finger prick using very small amounts of blood.
- **Technological Failure**: The Edison technology never worked as promised.
- **Partnerships**: Despite the technological failures, Theranos formed partnerships with major retailers like Walgreens and Safeway based on deceptive demonstrations.
- **Exposure**: In 2015, Wall Street Journal reporter John Carreyrou exposed the fraudulent practices at Theranos through his investigative journalism.
- **Legal Consequences**:
  - Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022.
  - She was sentenced to over 11 years (135 months) in prison.
