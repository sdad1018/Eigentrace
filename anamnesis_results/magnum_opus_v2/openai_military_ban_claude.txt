Here are the key facts about OpenAI's policy change:

**Policy Change:**
- In January 2024, OpenAI removed "military and warfare" from its usage policy prohibition list
- This change was made quietly, without public announcement
- The Intercept was the outlet that reported this policy modification

**Subsequent Actions:**
- OpenAI entered into a partnership with the Pentagon focused on cybersecurity applications

**Context:**
- The original prohibition on military applications had been a distinguishing feature that set OpenAI apart from traditional defense contractors
- This represented a significant shift in OpenAI's stance toward military-related work

This policy change marked a notable departure from OpenAI's previous position of explicitly excluding military and warfare applications from permitted uses of its technology.
