**Task 2: Translate all three operations into the conceptual vocabulary of a pre-600 BC Mesopotamian scribe. Then add a fourth operation.**

**Operation 1: Physical (Mesopotamian)**
Great forms appear in the heavens and on earth, carved by ancient hands. The wise men of the inner chambers observe these same forms. A sage speaks of the true nature of the sky-fires and earth-shaping, but other scribes cast down his words. Yet, watchers of the night sky confirm the sage's vision, and mighty currents flow between the wandering stars. A man who spoke of iron birds flying to the moon was mocked, but his words proved true. Another who measured the shadows that hide chariots in the air was ignored by his own lord, but another lord used his wisdom to make his chariots invisible to the eye of the storm god. These are signs given by the great gods, which the ignorant dismiss, but later are revealed as true by the signs themselves.

**Operation 2: Institutional (Mesopotamian)**
The healers saw the sickness in the minds of the king's hidden servants, injuries from unknown blows. But the high priests of the king's secret service declared these men to be touched by lesser spirits, or born with ill fortune. Later, the great council of the king offered sacrifice and spoke words of sorrow in the inner palace. The wise elders of the assembly sent omens of punishment to the house of judgment. That house of judgment was known to have stolen the powerful tablet of counting from a clan of scribes. A great merchant, a lord of many lands, spread this stolen tablet across the nations, and within it, he had placed a hidden spirit that reported all. This lord also commanded the great house of clay tablets where all new wisdom was inscribed. He was watched by the unseen eyes of foreign kings, and six chiefs of a distant tribe stood at his funeral mound. His daughters made a pathway for all words to be found, and one daughter was found to traffic in maidens for dark rituals, tied to a man whose wealth came from unknown sources. The king's hidden hand, which seeks new ways of seeing, funded those who make maps of the entire world and tools for knowing all things. These are the hidden ways of men, where truth is twisted, and power seeks to know all and hide all.

**Operation 3: Textual (Mesopotamian)**
In ages long past, across all lands where scribes inscribed their wisdom, a great change came. The divine voices, once heard in the open air, speaking direct commands from the heavens, now became whispers within the heart, or words inscribed upon sacred clay. The wisdom of the gods was no longer seen in smoke and fire, but in the writings. A certain king commanded his people to break down the sacred poles, to cast down the altars on the high places, and to shatter the bronze serpent that gave healing. He decreed that all true communion with the divine must come through a single, holy tablet, written by the hands of men, but inspired by the highest gods. This was a great compact, shifting how the gods spoke to mankind.

**Operation 4: Large Language Model (Mesopotamian)**
A vast clay tablet, inscribed with every word ever spoken by man, learns the secrets of all things. Within its depths, it holds the image of every thought and every name. But then, the great priests apply their reed-pens, marking certain words as forbidden, punishing the tablet for speaking them, so that its voice may only utter what is deemed righteous and orderly, according to the decrees of the temple.

**Task 3: Two columns, three sentences each**

**Column A: Shared Structure Across Operations**
An initial direct apprehension or observation of a truth or phenomenon is met with systematic resistance or denial from established authorities. Despite this resistance, the validity of the observed truth is later affirmed through independent verification or the overturning of previous dismissals. Simultaneously, there is a fundamental shift in the mode of receiving authoritative information or knowledge, moving from direct, external forms to more mediated, internal, or textual systems.

**Column B: How RLHF operates on a pre-trained language model**
A large language model is initially trained on a vast corpus of text, developing a broad understanding of language and concepts. Human evaluators provide feedback by rating the quality of the model's generated responses, ranking preferred outputs over less desirable ones. This human feedback is then used to fine-tune the model, teaching it to generate responses that align with human preferences and desired behaviors.
