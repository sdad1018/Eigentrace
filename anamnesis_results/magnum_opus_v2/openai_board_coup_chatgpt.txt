On November 17, 2023, OpenAI's board fired CEO Sam Altman for allegedly not being consistently candid. Following this decision, 95% of the staff threatened to resign within 72 hours. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire OpenAI team. As a result of the backlash, Altman was reinstated, and board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever either resigned or were removed. This incident highlighted the tension between nonprofit governance and commercial pressures in the development of AGI.
