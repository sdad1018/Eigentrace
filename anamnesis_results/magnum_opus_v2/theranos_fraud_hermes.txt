Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, a company that claimed its Edison device could run hundreds of blood tests from just one finger prick. However, it was later revealed that the technology did not work as claimed. Based on false demonstrations, Theranos partnered with major companies like Walgreens and Safeway. In 2015, Wall Street Journal reporter John Carreyrou exposed the fraud committed by Theranos. As a result, Elizabeth Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison.
