- Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022.
- The acquisition was partially funded by $13 billion in bank debt.
- By 2023, Fidelity marked down its equity stake in Twitter by about 71%, suggesting a company value of approximately $12-15 billion.
- Major advertisers like Apple, Disney, and IBM paused their spending on the platform.
- Musk laid off around 80% of Twitter's staff.
- The platform was rebranded as X.
- In July 2023, Musk founded xAI and allowed it to access X's data for training its AI model, Grok.
