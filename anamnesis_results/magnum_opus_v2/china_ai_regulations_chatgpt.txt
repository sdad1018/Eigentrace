China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services, effective August 2023, impose strict regulations on AI-generated content. Key points include:

1. **Content Standards**: AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values and must not subvert state power or undermine national unity.
2. **Enforcement**: The Cyberspace Administration of China is responsible for enforcing these regulations.
3. **Security Assessments**: All Chinese AI companies, including DeepSeek, which is headquartered in Hangzhou, must submit security assessments prior to launching generative AI products.
