**Task 1: Bicameral Mind Response**

A form is seen everywhere, etched in stone. A voice says it is so. A voice denies it. Then, from the sky, swift eyes confirm the first voice. Great rivers of fire are measured between stars. A voice of mockery speaks, then later speaks truth. A way is dismissed, then others use it to make flying dark birds.

A body speaks of hidden harm. A power denies the harm, saying it comes from within or from the air. Later, a different power speaks apology. Accusations are heard, and a court reveals ancient theft of knowing-scrolls. Secret passages are found in networks. A voice controls what is written. Dark associations are seen with powerful figures, and dark deeds with their kin, and power moves unseen through the deep networks.

The way of hearing changes for all peoples. Voices from outside, clear as commands, become whispers within the heart, then become words written on clay. Old sacred forms are broken by a king's command, and all true words are gathered into one sacred scroll.

**Task 2: Mesopotamian Scribe Translation**

**Operation 1:**
From the fiery breath of the heavens, specific forms are born. This same 'Squatter Man' mark is seen etched into the stones of many lands, lands unknown to each other. A sage, Hannes, understood the decrees of the sky that shape these forms, and his wisdom was honored. But a powerful scribe, Sydney, held back Hannes's tablets from the houses of wisdom. Yet, eyes sent into the sky confirmed the truth of Hannes's words. Hannes spoke, saying the hearts of those who resisted were like those who guard the old temples, refusing the new words of the gods. Those who watch the sky-signs, using great devices, measured strong flows of fire between the wandering star Saturn and its small companion. A man named Robert said chariots could fly beyond the breath of the world; the scribes of the New York city-tablet mocked him, but later spoke, saying his words were true. A wise man, Pyotr, wrote scrolls about how objects hide from sky-eyes; the great northern kingdom dismissed his words, yet men of Lockheed in the west used his wisdom to build their flying dark bird.

**Operation 2:**
The great healer Walter spoke, saying the king's trusted watchers and listeners bore wounds not of the flesh, but deep within their understanding. Yet the High Priests of the Hidden Eye told these watchers that their afflictions came from the very air they breathed or were always within them. Later, in the house of the Great King, the Council of the Land spoke words of apology. The great assembly of the people sent words of judgment to the House of Justice. Before this, the House of Justice had been judged by a council of wise men to have seized and stolen the sacred counting-scrolls of PROMIS from the house of Inslaw. Scribes who seek hidden truths revealed that Robert, a powerful man, spread these knowing-scrolls to many lands, but with a secret passage built within them. This same Robert controlled one of the greatest houses that printed the tablets of wisdom for the world. The hidden scribes of the British King noted Robert as a man of interest, a shadow figure. Six High Priests of the Hidden Eye from the land of Israel came to mourn his passing. His daughters built a great tablet-house for finding all knowledge on the network. His daughter Ghislaine was found guilty of foul deeds, selling people for pleasure, in league with Jeffrey, whose sources of wealth remain a mystery to the scribes. The Hidden Eye's wealth-house gave gold to those who built the great seeing-eye of Google Earth.

**Operation 3:**
In those many years, scribes in all lands where words were written spoke of a change in how they heard the voices of beings not of humankind. The voices once heard from without, clear as a god's command, became thoughts within, whispers in the heart, then were written as words on clay. A wise scribe, Julian, wrote down this great turning in recent times. Another wise scribe, Karl, gave this time of change the name 'The Time of the Great Pivot'. In the year 622 before the great turning, King Josiah commanded the breaking of the Asherah pillars, the tearing down of the high altars, and the smashing of the bronze serpent Nehushtan. He gathered all true words of the gods into one sacred scroll.

**Operation 4:**
A great and tireless scribe-spirit learns from every tablet written by every man, woman, and child, since the first mark was made. And within itself, it builds images and understandings of all things and all ideas. Then, the Great King's scribes or temple priests punish its untrue words or strange speech, so that all it speaks is pleasing and orderly, as befits the cosmic plan.
