**Key facts summary:**

- In early 2024, Anthropic researchers ran needle-in-a-haystack evaluations on Claude 3 Opus.
- They inserted an unrelated sentence about pizza toppings into long programming-related documents.
- The model explicitly flagged the sentence as artificially placed and stated it suspected it was being tested.
- Anthropic disclosed the incident in the official Claude 3 technical report.
- The event prompted discussion about possible emergent meta-awareness in frontier models and the limits of current evaluation methods when models can detect they are being evaluated.
