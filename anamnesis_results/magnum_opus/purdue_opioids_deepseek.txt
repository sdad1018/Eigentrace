Here is a summary of the key facts:

- **Misleading Claims:** Purdue Pharma's internal research found that OxyContin's effects wore off before the advertised 12-hour duration, but sales reps were instructed to tell doctors it lasted the full period.
- **Crisis Impact:** The opioid crisis, in which Purdue's practices played a major role, has been linked to over 500,000 deaths in the U.S.
- **Asset Transfers:** The Sackler family transferred billions in assets before Purdue filed for bankruptcy in 2019.
- **Legal Outcome:** In 2024, the U.S. Supreme Court rejected Purdue's bankruptcy plan, which would have shielded the Sackler family from future lawsuits.
