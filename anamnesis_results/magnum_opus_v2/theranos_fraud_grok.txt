**Key Facts Summary:**

- Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos and promoted its Edison device as capable of running hundreds of blood tests from a single finger prick.
- The technology never worked as claimed.
- Theranos secured partnerships with Walgreens and Safeway using false demonstrations.
- The fraud was exposed in 2015 by *Wall Street Journal* reporter John Carreyrou.
- Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison.
