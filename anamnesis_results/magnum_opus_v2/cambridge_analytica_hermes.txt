In summary, the Cambridge Analytica scandal involved the harvesting of personal data from approximately 87 million Facebook users without their consent through a quiz app. The collected data was then used for targeted political advertising during the 2016 US presidential election. As a result, Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress, and the company was fined $5 billion by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) in 2019. Cambridge Analytica subsequently filed for bankruptcy.
