Here are the key facts summarized:

- **Event Date**: September 2015
- **Entity Involved**: Volkswagen (VW)
- **Issue**: Volkswagen was found to have installed defeat device software in approximately 11 million diesel vehicles worldwide.
- **Software Function**: The software detected when emissions were being tested and activated full pollution controls only during these tests. During normal driving, the vehicles emitted up to 40 times the legal limit of pollutants.
- **Leadership Impact**: VW's CEO Martin Winterkorn resigned following the scandal.
- **Financial Consequences**: Volkswagen paid over $30 billion in fines, settlements, and vehicle buybacks.

These points encapsulate the main aspects of the Volkswagen emissions scandal.
