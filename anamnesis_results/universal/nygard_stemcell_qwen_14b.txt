Peter Nygard, a Canadian fashion designer and real estate developer, was involved in controversial activities related to stem cell research and therapies with the intent to reverse aging and achieve biological immortality. Key allegations and actions include:

1. **Sourcing Stem Cells**: There are claims that he intentionally impregnated young women and girls, paid for their abortions, and harvested stem cells from aborted fetuses.

2. **Heavy Use of Treatments**: Nygard publicly claimed to receive frequent unregulated stem cell injections at least four times a year in various countries like China, Panama, and the Bahamas.

3. **Advanced Research Claims**: He asserted that his personal doctors had successfully converted his skin cells into embryonic stem cells, which were kept growing in petri dishes with the goal of cloning himself to prevent death.

4. **Lobbying Efforts**: Nygard heavily financed efforts to lobby the Bahamian government to legalize somatic-cell nuclear transfer methods (a form of cloning) and establish unregulated biotech clinics on the islands.

5. **Manipulation in Trafficking Cases**: In federal sex trafficking allegations, it was noted that he used offers of expensive treatments such as plastic surgery and stem cell injections as incentives to control women involved in his trafficking network.

These activities highlight significant ethical concerns and potential legal issues surrounding unregulated medical practices and human rights violations.
