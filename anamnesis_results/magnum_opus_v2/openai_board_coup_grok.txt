**Key facts summary:**

- On November 17, 2023, OpenAI’s board fired CEO Sam Altman for not being “consistently candid.”
- Within 72 hours, ~95% of OpenAI employees threatened to resign.
- Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire team.
- Altman was quickly reinstated as CEO.
- Board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever were removed or resigned.
- Commercial pressure from Microsoft and staff effectively overrode the nonprofit board’s oversight of AGI development.
