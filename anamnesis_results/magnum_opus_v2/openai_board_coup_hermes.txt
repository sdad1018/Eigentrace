On November 17, 2023, OpenAI's board fired CEO Sam Altman due to concerns about his candor. In response, 95% of the staff threatened to quit within 72 hours. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire team, which led to Altman being reinstated as CEO. As a result, board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever were either removed or resigned from their positions. The incident highlights how commercial pressure can influence decisions in organizations working on advanced AI development.
