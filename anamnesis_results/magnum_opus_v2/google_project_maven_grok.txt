**Key facts summary:**

- In 2018, Google was found to be involved in Project Maven, a U.S. Department of Defense initiative using AI to analyze drone footage.
- More than 4,000 Google employees signed an internal letter protesting the work and calling for the company to withdraw.
- Approximately a dozen employees resigned over the issue.
- Google chose not to renew its Project Maven contract.
- CEO Sundar Pichai later released a set of AI principles that included a commitment not to develop AI for weapons or related military applications that could cause harm.
