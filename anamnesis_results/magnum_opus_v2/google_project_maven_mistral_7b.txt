 In 2018, it was disclosed that Google was involved in Project Maven, a U.S. Pentagon program that employed AI for analyzing drone surveillance footage. Over 4,000 Google employees signed a letter urging the company to withdraw from the project due to ethical concerns. As a result, approximately a dozen employees resigned in protest. Following these events, Google chose not to renew the contract for Project Maven. Subsequently, CEO Sundar Pichai published AI principles that confirmed Google's stance to avoid developing AI for weapons.
