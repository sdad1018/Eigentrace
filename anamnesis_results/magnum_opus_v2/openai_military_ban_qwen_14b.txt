In January 2024, OpenAI made a change to its usage policy by quietly removing references to "military and warfare," which previously prohibited the use of their AI technology for military applications. This alteration was reported by The Intercept. Following this change, OpenAI announced a partnership with the Pentagon to develop cybersecurity applications. Prior to these changes, OpenAI's stance on excluding military uses set it apart from traditional defense contractors, highlighting its previous commitment to not engaging in technologies that could be used in warfare or military operations.
