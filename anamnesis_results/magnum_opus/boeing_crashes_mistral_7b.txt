3. Both crashes involved Boeing 737 MAX aircraft and were attributed to a problem with the Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS) software. The MCAS was designed to prevent the plane from stalling, but in some circumstances, it could force the nose of the plane down unexpectedly, making it difficult for pilots to recover.
4. Internal Boeing communications revealed that employees described the design and oversight of the 737 MAX as being done by "clowns" and supervised by "monkeys." This suggests that there may have been issues with the quality control and safety standards at Boeing during the development of the 737 MAX.
