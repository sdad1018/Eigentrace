Here are the key facts summarized:

- **Timnit Gebru**, co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team, was fired in December 2020.
- The firing occurred after she co-authored a paper titled 'On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots,' which raised concerns about environmental costs and bias in large language models.
- Google stated that the paper had not passed its internal review process.
- Gebru claimed she was terminated for raising these concerns.
- **Margaret Mitchell**, the other co-lead of the Ethical AI team, was also subsequently fired.
- Multiple researchers resigned in protest following these events.
