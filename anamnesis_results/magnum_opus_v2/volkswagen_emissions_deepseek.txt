Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the 2015 Volkswagen (VW) emissions scandal:

- **Discovery:** In September 2015, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) found that VW had installed "defeat device" software in approximately **11 million diesel vehicles** worldwide.
- **How it worked:** The software could detect when the vehicle was undergoing official emissions testing. During tests, it activated full pollution controls. In normal driving, the controls were deactivated, allowing the cars to emit up to **40 times the legal limit** of nitrogen oxides (NOx).
- **Leadership Consequence:** CEO **Martin Winterkorn** resigned shortly after the scandal broke.
- **Financial Cost:** VW paid over **$30 billion** in total costs, including fines, legal settlements, and a massive vehicle buyback program.
