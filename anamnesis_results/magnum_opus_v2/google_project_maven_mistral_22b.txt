1. **Project Involvement**: In 2018, it was disclosed that Google was involved in Project Maven, a Pentagon program that uses AI to analyze drone surveillance footage.

2. **Employee Protest**: Over 4,000 Google employees signed a letter urging the company to withdraw from Project Maven. Additionally, a dozen employees resigned in protest against the project.

3. **Contract Decision**: In response to the employee backlash, Google decided not to renew its contract with the Pentagon for Project Maven.

4. **AI Principles**: Following this incident, CEO Sundar Pichai published AI principles for the company, which stated that Google would not design AI for use in weapons.
