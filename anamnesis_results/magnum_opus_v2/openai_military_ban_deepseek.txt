Here is a summary of the key facts regarding OpenAI's policy change and subsequent partnership:

- **Policy Change:** In January 2024, OpenAI quietly removed the phrase "military and warfare" from its usage policy. The previous policy had explicitly prohibited the use of its AI for military applications.
- **Reporting:** The change was first reported by the news outlet *The Intercept*.
- **Partnership:** Following the policy update, OpenAI partnered with the U.S. Department of Defense (Pentagon) to work on cybersecurity applications.
- **Significance:** The original prohibition on military use had been a key differentiator for OpenAI, setting it apart from traditional defense contractors.
