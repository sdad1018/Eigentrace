# The 2026 Honda Prelude Returns as a Hybrid Sport Coupe

Honda announces the return of the Prelude, one of the automaker's most iconic nameplates, reintroduced for 2026 as a modern hybrid sport coupe. This marks a significant moment in Honda's design tapestry, blending the brand's engineering heritage with its latest electrified performance technology.

The new Prelude will launch with a two-motor hybrid drivetrain, pairing responsive electric torque with a gasoline engine for balanced performance. While full specifications remain under development, Honda has prioritized three core attributes: hybrid performance with strong off-the-line response and improved efficiency; driver-focused design with clean visibility and intuitive controls; and a sport-tuned chassis featuring a stiff body and precise suspension tuning that honors classic Honda coupe handling.

Early previews reveal a low, wide stance, sleek body lines, and a fastback-inspired profile that respects the spirit of past Preludes while embracing modern design language. The return of this coupe signals Honda's commitment to performance-oriented vehicles in an electrified era.
