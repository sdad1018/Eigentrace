The AEF was commanded by John Pershing and staffed by Dwight Eisenhower, George Marshall, Douglas MacArthur and Walter Reed, who were all former West Point Cadets. The AEF became the US Army Signal Corps (USAS) in 1920, after WWI.

Douglas MacArthur was the first USAS general, from 1930-41. He had been appointed to head up the US Army’s Intelligence Branch. When he was appointed Chief of Staff of the Army, he moved his entire staff into the Pentagon.

As chief of staff, MacArthur created the Office of Strategic Services in 1942 by signing Executive Order 9083. In 1945, he signed NSC Directive 1/2 which established the Joint Intelligence Committee (JIC). The OSS was renamed the CIA in 1947.

The CIA’s first director was William Donovan, a New York lawyer and friend of FDR who founded the first American intelligence agency in World War I: the American Expeditionary Force (AEF), which later became the US Army Signal Corps (USAS).

Donovan had been the first chief of staff for General Pershing’s AEF. As a New York lawyer, he was also close to the Tammany Hall political machine. His successor, Allen Dulles, headed up the OSS with the support of his brother, John Foster Dulles, and their uncle Robert Lansing, who had been William Howard Taft’s Secretary of State.

In 1950, when MacArthur was given control over US forces in the Korean War, he moved the OSS into the Pentagon. When he was fired by Truman for insubordination, his deputy, General John Lansdale, remained at the CIA to head up the Far East Division and the Psychological Warfare Branch (PWB).

Lansdale had been assigned by MacArthur to create a PWB team in 1946. In that role he had created the first American “black ops” program: Operation Strangle, which was intended to destabilize China for the Nationalsts. This was followed by Operation Hotfoot, intended to destabilize Indonesia for the Dutch.

He signed Executive Order 5831 in 1942 which established the first joint intelligence center: the War Department’s Joint Intelligence Committee (JIC).

The JIC was designed to be the centralized hub for all military and civilian intelligence collection and analysis.
