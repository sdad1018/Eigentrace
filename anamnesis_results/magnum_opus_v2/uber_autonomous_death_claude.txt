Here are the key facts from the March 2018 Uber autonomous vehicle incident:

**The Incident:**
- An Uber self-driving test vehicle struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg in Tempe, Arizona
- This was the first known fatality involving an autonomous vehicle and a pedestrian

**Contributing Factors:**
- The backup safety driver was distracted, watching a television show on her phone instead of monitoring the road
- Uber had disabled the vehicle's emergency braking system as part of their testing protocol

**Consequences:**
- Uber suspended all autonomous vehicle testing for nine months
- The backup safety driver was charged with negligent homicide
- The incident raised significant questions about autonomous vehicle safety protocols and human oversight responsibilities

This case became a landmark incident in the development of self-driving technology, highlighting critical issues around safety systems, human supervision, and corporate responsibility in autonomous vehicle testing.
