In October 2022, Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion, partly funded by $13 billion in bank debt. By 2023, Fidelity marked down its equity stake by approximately 71%, implying a total company value around $12-15 billion. Major advertisers paused spending, and Musk laid off about 80% of Twitter's staff. The platform was renamed X, and Musk founded xAI in July 2023, giving it access to X's data for training Grok.
