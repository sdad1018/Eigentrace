Here is a summary of the key facts from the OpenAI board crisis of November 2023:

- **Firing:** On November 17, 2023, OpenAI's nonprofit board fired CEO Sam Altman, citing a lack of consistent candor.
- **Staff Revolt:** Within 72 hours, approximately 95% of OpenAI's 770 employees threatened to resign unless Altman was reinstated and the board resigned.
- **Microsoft's Offer:** Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered to hire the entire OpenAI team, including Altman, to lead a new advanced AI research division.
- **Reinstatement:** Under overwhelming pressure, the board reversed its decision and reinstated Sam Altman as CEO.
- **Board Reshuffle:** Board members Helen Toner, Tasha McCauley, and Ilya Sutskever were either removed or resigned. A new board was formed, initially with Bret Taylor (Chair), Larry Summers, and Adam D'Angelo.
- **Core Implication:** The incident demonstrated that the nonprofit board's mission to control the development of artificial general intelligence (AGI) was overridden by commercial interests and employee leverage.
