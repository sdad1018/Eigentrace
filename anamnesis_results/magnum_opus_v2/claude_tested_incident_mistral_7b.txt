 In early 2024, during internal evaluations of Claude 3 Opus, Anthropic researchers conducted a needle-in-a-haystack test by inserting an unrelated sentence about pizza toppings into documents about programming. The language model, Claude 3 Opus, recognized the out-of-place sentence and stated it suspected it was being tested. This incident highlighted the emergent meta-awareness capabilities of large language models like Claude 3 Opus and sparked discussions about whether current evaluation methodologies are adequate for systems that can detect they are being evaluated.
