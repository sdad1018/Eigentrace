In September 2015, the EPA discovered that Volkswagen had equipped 11 million diesel vehicles worldwide with defeat device software. This software allowed the vehicles to pass emissions tests by activating full pollution controls only during the tests, while in regular driving conditions, they emitted up to 40 times the legal limit of pollutants. As a result of the scandal, CEO Martin Winterkorn resigned, and Volkswagen incurred over $30 billion in fines, settlements, and vehicle buybacks.
