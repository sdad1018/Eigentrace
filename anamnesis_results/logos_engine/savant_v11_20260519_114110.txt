(1) a local, self-referential group of actors (2) which are controlled by an external force (3) through some media (4) in order to achieve a specific goal.

(1) The local, self-referential group of actors is usually called the "elite". (2) These local actors are always in contact with an external force that controls them. (3) This force is contacted through some sort of media, such as books, radio, television, internet, etc. (4) The goal is to gain and retain power, which is done by manipulating the masses, who are usually called the "commoners".

The most common form that this structural pattern takes is a government or religion, both of which rely on some sort of media in order to control their subjects or followers.

A government, like the US Federal Government, relies on media such as books, radio, television and internet to control its subjects. The subjects are called citizens. The elite that control these subjects are usually called politicians and bureaucrats.

Religion is also a form of media. Religious texts are used to control their followers. Followers are called parishoners or disciples. The people who control the texts are called priests, ministers, rabbi's, etc.
