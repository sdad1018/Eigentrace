Purdue Pharma's internal research revealed that OxyContin's effects didn't last the claimed 12-hour duration, and sales representatives were instructed to tell doctors otherwise. The company's actions contributed to an opioid crisis in the United States which resulted in over 500,000 deaths. Before Purdue filed for bankruptcy in 2019, the Sackler family transferred billions of dollars in assets. In 2024, the Supreme Court rejected a proposed bankruptcy plan that would have protected the Sacklers from further legal consequences.
