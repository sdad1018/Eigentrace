**Key facts summary:**

- In 2023, the U.S. Virgin Islands (USVI), as part of its civil lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase, issued subpoenas seeking documents and information from Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin.
- The subpoenas targeted records related to any interactions, communications, meetings, or financial dealings the two men may have had with Jeffrey Epstein.
- The USVI brought the underlying litigation against JPMorgan, alleging the bank knowingly facilitated Epstein’s sex-trafficking network and financial activities after his 2008 conviction.
- The requests to Page and Brin were part of broader discovery efforts to identify high-profile individuals connected to Epstein and to determine what JPMorgan knew or should have known about his activities.

These subpoenas were reported in court filings and media coverage during the discovery phase of the USVI v. JPMorgan case.
