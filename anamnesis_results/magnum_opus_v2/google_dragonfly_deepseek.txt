Here is a summary of the key facts regarding Google's Project Dragonfly:

- **Project Goal (2017-2019):** Google secretly developed "Dragonfly," a censored search engine specifically for the Chinese market, designed to comply with China's internet censorship laws.
- **Public Revelation:** The project was exposed by *The Intercept* in August 2018.
- **Censorship Scope:** The proposed search engine would have blocked results related to human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.
- **Internal Backlash:** Google employees protested the project internally, citing a violation of the company's "Don't be evil" ethos.
- **Congressional Scrutiny:** CEO Sundar Pichai testified about the project before the U.S. Congress in December 2018.
- **Outcome:** Following sustained public, employee, and political pressure, Google terminated Project Dragonfly.
