1. Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services: The measures are issued by China to regulate the content generated by AI services, and they will be effective from August 2023. These rules aim to ensure that AI-generated content adheres to core socialist values.

2. Prohibition on subversive content: According to these measures, AI-generated content must not contain any material that undermines or subverts state power in China. This is an attempt by the Chinese government to maintain its authority and control over information dissemination.

3. Preservation of national unity: The Interim Measures also require that generative AI services should not produce content that could potentially harm national unity in China. This can be seen as a step taken by the government to prevent any potential divisions within society.

4. Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC): CAC is responsible for enforcing these measures and ensuring compliance among Chinese companies operating in the AI sector. As the regulatory body overseeing internet-related matters, it plays a crucial role in implementing these rules.

5. DeepSeek: A Chinese AI company headquartered in Hangzhou, operates under this regulatory framework imposed by the Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services. All AI companies in China must adhere to these rules and guidelines to continue their operations within the country.

6. Security assessments: Before launching any generative AI products, Chinese AI companies like DeepSeek must submit security assessments to ensure that their products align with the specified regulatory requirements and do not pose a threat to core socialist values or national unity in China.
