Here is a summary of the key facts regarding the Boeing 737 MAX crashes:

- **Two Crashes:** Lion Air Flight 610 (October 2018, 189 killed) and Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 (March 2019, 157 killed) resulted in a total of 346 deaths.
- **Root Cause:** Both crashes were caused by the flawed design and activation of the Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS) software on the Boeing 737 MAX.
- **Internal Culture:** Internal Boeing communications revealed employees had described the plane as "designed by clowns who in turn are supervised by monkeys," indicating a toxic safety culture and disregard for proper oversight.
- **Legal Outcome:** In January 2021, Boeing agreed to pay a total of $2.5 billion to settle charges of conspiracy to defraud the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).
