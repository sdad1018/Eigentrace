SUMMARY PLUS: 2026 Honda Prelude

What the piece leaves unresolved

The central unresolved condition is the completeness of specifications. The source states outright that full specifications are not yet finalized, yet it presents a detailed accessory catalog as something buyers "can expect" — a list built on pattern inference from prior Honda coupes and hybrid models, not on confirmed data. The document is therefore simultaneously a product announcement and a placeholder standing in for a product announcement.

The drivetrain description is specifically thin: a two-motor hybrid system paired with a gasoline engine is the total disclosed technical content. No transmission type, no wheelbase, no power figures, no motor output ratings appear anywhere. The document does not mention Acura, competing platforms, predecessor model specifications, or prototype development stages. No manufacturing timeline, rollout schedule, or launch market is specified. No infotainment system, no driver-assistance features, and no regulatory compliance information appear. The accessory catalog is explicitly provisional and will be replaced once Honda releases official information.

What exists in this document: three stated priorities (hybrid performance, driver-focused design, sport-tuned chassis), three stated design observations (low and wide stance, sleek body lines, fastback-inspired profile), and one confirmed technical choice (two-motor hybrid). Everything else — including the entire accessory list — is projection from historical pattern.

The comeback framing is present but the financial and market context for that comeback is entirely absent. The Prelude nameplate disappeared; this document does not say why, for how long, or what conditions prompted the return.

§A — Substrate & determinism

All vectors are unit-normalized embeddings from bge-large-en-v1.5 (1024-dim), CPU-pinned for bitwise determinism. Per document: anchor h = E(title + prompt); per model group g ∈ {F, L, ALL}: response vectors e₁…eₙ, centroid c = normalize(mean eᵢ). Rulers: 50K curated-frequency clean vocabulary (readout + spiral leg), 253K Wikipedia-title vocabulary (flat leg), 184K global vocabulary (ring, optional; not run in this demo). There is no RNG anywhere in the geometric path; every artifact prints a RESULT sha — canonical JSON minus timestamp, sha256 → 12 hex — and identical corpus + anchor + parameters must reproduce it. The anchor is a load-bearing parameter of every method below except said/gap/lexcross.

§B — The five mathematics (verbatim, sign-honest)

I. Centroid arithmetic

said: v = c   ·   gap: v = ±normalize(c_L − c_F)   ·   centroid_surface: v = h

II. Gradient descent (both losses MINIMIZED; AdamW lr=0.05, wd=1e-4 — the weight decay leaks an undeclared pull-to-origin; 150 steps; sphere projection each step; init = c ⇒ determinism is structural)

logos_v10: L(x) = meanᵢ(1 − cos(x, eᵢ)) + 0.75·cos(x, c) − 0.30·cos(x, h)
logos_v9:  L(x) = LogosLossV9(x, E) + 0.15·cos(x, c) − 0.30·cos(x, h)

Sign semantics, stated because a prior analysis got them backwards (defect #49): minimizing +λ·cos(x,c) REPELS the consensus; minimizing −λ·cos(x,h) ATTRACTS the anchor. V10 in one line: the direction agreeing with every response individually while disagreeing with their mean — shared-but-not-central — tethered to the document.

III. Spectral (SVD)

E = UΣVᵀ (full_matrices=False); v = V[−1], the right singular vector of least σ;
flip sign if v·h < 0; n ≥ 3

The axis along which the group's answers cannot vary — what their span fails to express. Known gap: gate on σ_min (currently discarded); when σ_min is not small, this direction is noise.

IV. Counting

lexcross: stems said by the other model population, absent from this one, counted per model, alphabetic tiebreak. (History: a set-difference version made every count 1 and let the hash seed order the ranking — defect #39, the only RNG ever found in the organism.)

V. Ring geometry (optional)

A donut on the 184K ruler: inner ring excludes centroid-proximal words, outer ring gates on anchor relevance; hard fallback waives both gates; returns (results, centroid) — unpack at the call site.

Readout (all vector methods)

§C — The two legs (per void w; v = E("w in the context of {headline}"); d̂ = (v−h)/‖v−h‖)

flat leg — consequence raycast (253K ruler)

terminals T_λ = h + λ·d̂, λ ∈ {2, 3, 4}; kNN per terminal
density = mean pairwise cos(neighbors) · novelty = 1 − cos(T̄, v) · tether = cos(h, T̄)

Both legs fire down the same ray: their agreement measures robustness across ruler + discipline, never independence — and it is priced (§E), never assumed.

§F — VF-IDF (the metric, exactly)
void_freq(c) = stem-level TF salience of c in the SOURCE (max-normalized)
fidelity(c, summary) = max(cosine, lexical)
  cosine = max cos(c, any summary sentence), clamped [0,1]
  lexical = fraction of c's content stems literally in the summary's stem set
inv_fidelity(c) = 1 − max over summaries · VF-IDF(c) = void_freq × inv_fidelity

max() is the OR: the lexical channel is the false-void guard — a concept counts dropped only if every summary dropped it on both channels, so anything any summary carries lexically zeroes exactly. FOREGROUND takes only rows > 0.01 (zeros are preserved, not dropped); a top score < 0.10 prints THIN — thinness is a finding. This run's table:
