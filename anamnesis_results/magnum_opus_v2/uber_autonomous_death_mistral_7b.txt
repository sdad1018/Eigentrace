 In March 2018, a fatal accident involving an Uber autonomous test vehicle occurred in Tempe, Arizona. Elaine Herzberg, a pedestrian, was struck and killed by the self-driving Uber car. At the time of the incident, the backup safety driver was reportedly watching a television show on her phone instead of focusing on the road. It was also disclosed that Uber's self-driving program had previously disabled the vehicle's emergency braking system.

Following the accident, Uber suspended autonomous testing for nine months as part of an investigation and review process. The safety driver involved in the incident was later charged with negligent homicide due to her lack of attention while operating the vehicle.
