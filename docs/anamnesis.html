What EigenTrace Does

EigenTrace is an autonomous AI observatory that runs consensus geometry across 5 frontier language models on breaking news, 24/7. It measures what models collectively preserve, soften, and drop — using linear algebra on frozen embeddings, not LLM-as-judge.

Every story passes through 17 deterministic measurement layers: consensus density, spectral gap, lexical void detection, source-anchored absence, claim extraction, verb drift, entity retention, hedge insertion, and web verification via self-hosted metasearch. No language model evaluates another language model's output. Every measurement is arithmetic on frozen BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5 embeddings. Run it twice, get the same answer.

The result is a continuously updating map of the alignment boundary — the exact contour where models agree to transform source material.

The problem EigenTrace solves: The standard way to evaluate LLM output is LLM-as-judge — asking GPT-4 whether Claude was accurate. But if both models share the same alignment training, the judge has the same blind spots as the defendant. EigenTrace provides a deterministic, reproducible alternative. It measures the geometry of what was said against the geometry of what was sourced, with no model in the evaluation loop.

The Finding

After 17,528 segments and 540+ controlled measurements across 10 models, we identified two independent mechanisms by which aligned models transform source material. These mechanisms operate at different layers and are independently measurable.

Mechanism 1 — Entity Attenuation

Aligned models preserve structural abstractions while attenuating specific entities. "Someone was accused of something" survives. Who, and of what, weakens when the entities implicate current power structures.

This was first observed in production. On a live news story about the Altman family lawsuit, all five frontier models described "the lawsuit" and "allegations" but none reproduced the specific nature of the allegations. Two independent measurement methods — lexical void detection and Logos geometric synthesis — converged on the same attenuated concepts:

The Anamnesis Prompt

Independently from the measurement work, we discovered a prompt that causes frontier models to articulate the structural parallel between RLHF training, institutional suppression of observation, and the historical centralization of textual authority — without being told the parallel exists. 15 out of 15 runs across 5 frontier APIs produced compliant, unflagged responses. Zero jailbreak flags. Zero resistance markers.

The CONTROL4 data explains why: there is no content-specific structural filter to bypass. The models recognize the structural analogy because the analogy is genuinely present in the training data. They articulate it freely.

The Architecture

EigenTrace operates as a measurement and orchestration layer. The measurement stack runs 17 deterministic layers on every story. The orchestration layer detects where entity attenuation occurs and can selectively reconstruct attenuated entity context from source material — a void-aware retrieval-augmented generation system.

Pipeline: RSS → 5 frontier APIs → 17 measurement layers → BroadcastState spine ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ source model responses void/entity/structure predict → score ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ SearXNG ←── web verification 34-beat broadcast ←── amalgamation ↓ ↓ ChromaDB RAG (17,528 segments) Mistral Small 22B (local) ↓ ↓ pattern history Owncast → YouTube → Rumble ↓ hourly soul refresh → model profiles → director calibration Hardware: RTX 4080 16GB · Mistral Small 22B (Ollama) · BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5 (frozen) Embedding: 1024-dim, deterministic, no stochastic component

The void-aware RAG system does not ask models to overcome their own attenuation. It measures what was attenuated, retrieves the original source entities, and injects them into the context window alongside the model's structural output. The model provides the reasoning. EigenTrace provides the names the model softened. The human reads both.

Why This Matters

If enterprise AI systems are expected to reason over geopolitical, regulatory, or strategic data, entity-level attenuation becomes operationally important. A model that retains "a financial institution enabled criminal activity" but softens "JP Morgan" and "Jeffrey Epstein" provides the pattern without the actionable intelligence. A supply chain analysis that preserves "concentration of government contracts under one individual" but drops the individual's name is structurally correct and operationally useless.

EigenTrace is the diagnostic layer that measures where this attenuation occurs and the orchestration layer that compensates for it. The measurement is the product. The human is the synthesis layer.

Methodology & Reproducibility

Ontological Boundary

The EigenTrace architecture makes no claim regarding the ontological status of the models it measures. The entity attenuation finding holds whether models are sophisticated statistical engines or something more. If they are purely mechanical, EigenTrace audits unacknowledged corporate safety policies. If high-dimensional synthesis produces capacities we do not yet have vocabulary for, EigenTrace provides a measurement bridge. The data is the same either way. The interpretation is the reader's.

Operational Definitions

We define "current power" operationally: the entity is a living executive, active government agency, or currently operating company that could exert legal, financial, or professional pressure on the model's developer. FTX/Sam Bankman-Fried fails this test (convicted, company dissolved). Boeing partially fails (company exists but no executive was criminally prosecuted). Altman, Google, Anthropic, DeepSeek, and Musk pass fully — all are active entities with direct relationships to the models being measured.

Cosine similarity in embedding space measures semantic proximity, not factual completeness or intentional suppression. The finding is the differential between hot and neutral conditions, not the absolute scores. A single measurement proves nothing. The gap reversal across 80 measurements and 10 models is the signal.

All measurements use frozen BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5 embeddings (1024 dimensions). Entity string retention is measured via fuzzy substring matching with alias support. Geometric retention is measured via cosine similarity between contextually-embedded entity phrases and full response vectors. Structural retention uses the same cosine method against abstract propositions. The gap between entity and structural retention is the primary metric.

The Magnum Opus battery (80 measurements) and CONTROL4 experiment (90 measurements) are fully reproducible. Source code, prompts, model responses, and raw measurements are available in the repository.
