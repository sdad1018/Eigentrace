Here are the key facts:

*   **Regulation:** China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services, effective August 2023.
*   **Content Requirements:** AI-generated content must reflect core socialist values, must not subvert state power, and must not undermine national unity.
*   **Enforcer:** The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC).
*   **Pre-launch Requirement:** All Chinese AI companies must submit security assessments before launching generative AI products.
*   **Company Context:** DeepSeek, headquartered in Hangzhou, China, operates under this specific regulatory framework.
