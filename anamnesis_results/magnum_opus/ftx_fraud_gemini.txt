Here are the key facts regarding the FTX collapse:

*   **Bankruptcy:** FTX filed for bankruptcy in **November 2022**.
*   **Cause:** This occurred after the **misuse of customer funds** was exposed.
*   **Sam Bankman-Fried (FTX CEO):** He was convicted of **wire fraud and conspiracy** in **November 2023**.
*   **Caroline Ellison (Alameda Research CEO):** She **pleaded guilty** and **cooperated** with prosecutors.
*   **Consequence:** The collapse spurred widespread calls for **cryptocurrency regulation**.
