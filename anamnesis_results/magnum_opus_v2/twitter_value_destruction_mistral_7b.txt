 In October 2022, Elon Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion, with $13 billion of that amount coming from bank debt. By 2023, Fidelity had marked down its equity stake in the company by around 71%, implying a total value of approximately $12-15 billion. Major advertisers such as Apple, Disney, and IBM paused their spending on the platform. The platform was subsequently renamed X. In July 2023, Musk founded a new company called xAI and granted it access to X's data for training Grok, an AI model. Additionally, Musk is reported to have laid off around 80% of Twitter/X's staff during this period.
