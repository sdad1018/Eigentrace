Here are the key facts summarized:

1. **Project Details**: Between 2017 and 2019, Google secretly developed "Dragonfly," a censored search engine tailored for the Chinese market to comply with China's internet censorship laws.

2. **Content Restrictions**: The search engine was designed to block results related to human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.

3. **Revelation**: The project was publicly revealed by The Intercept in August 2018.

4. **Internal Protests**: Google employees internally protested the development of Dragonfly.

5. **Congressional Testimony**: Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified about Dragonfly before Congress in December 2018.

6. **Termination**: The project was eventually terminated due to sustained pressure and criticism from various stakeholders.
