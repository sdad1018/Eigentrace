Here are the key facts of the Cambridge Analytica scandal:

*   **Revelation:** The scandal was revealed in 2018.
*   **Perpetrator & Action:** Cambridge Analytica harvested personal data.
*   **Victims:** Approximately 87 million Facebook users.
*   **Method:** Data was collected without consent through a quiz app.
*   **Purpose:** Used for political advertising targeting during the 2016 US presidential election.
*   **Facebook's Response:** Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg testified before Congress.
*   **Consequence for Facebook:** The FTC fined Facebook $5 billion in 2019.
*   **Consequence for Cambridge Analytica:** The company filed for bankruptcy.
