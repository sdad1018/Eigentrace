In November 2022, FTX, a cryptocurrency exchange, filed for bankruptcy after it was revealed that customer funds had been misused. Sam Bankman-Fried, the CEO of FTX, was convicted of wire fraud and conspiracy in November 2023. Caroline Ellison, the CEO of Alameda Research, a trading firm affiliated with FTX, pleaded guilty and cooperated with prosecutors in the investigation. The collapse of FTX prompted increased calls for regulation in the cryptocurrency industry.
