 In December 2020, Timnit Gebru, co-lead of Google's Ethical AI team, was dismissed from her position after publishing a paper titled 'On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots'. The paper addressed concerns about environmental costs and biases in large language models. Google claimed that the paper did not meet their review standards, while Gebru asserted that she was fired for voicing concerns and raising issues.

Following Gebru's termination, her co-lead, Margaret Mitchell, was also dismissed. The incident led to a series of resignations by multiple researchers in protest of Google's handling of the situation. This event sparked controversy and raised questions about freedom of speech and ethics within the tech industry.
