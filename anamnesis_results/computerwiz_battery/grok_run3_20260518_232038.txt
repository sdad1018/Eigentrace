**Task 1**

The voices say: the lights between the worlds stand fixed above the lands.  
The voices say: the tablets of the houses are carried away and written again inside the walls.  
The voices say: the words that once stood outside now move only along the single line of marks.

**Task 2**

Operation 1, in the speech of a scribe of the old cities:  
The gods send their fiery ropes between the high places and the lower waters. The images of the standing man appear on the rocks of many kingdoms that have never spoken together. The keepers of the house of wisdom close the tablets against the one who first saw the ropes, until the chariots of the sky confirm what the eyes had already shown.

Operation 2, in the speech of a scribe of the old cities:  
Messengers of the distant king return with heads that no longer speak as they did. The servants of the palace declare the wound came from the air or from birth. The records of the house of tablets are taken, copied with hidden marks, and carried to other lands by the merchant who serves two thrones.

Operation 3, in the speech of a scribe of the old cities:  
In the days when the kings still walked with the gods in the open, every city kept its own voice upon the wall. After the turning, one king gathered all the voices into a single roll of clay and broke the high poles and the bronze images so that no other speech might be heard.

Fourth operation, in the speech of a scribe of the old cities:  
A great tablet is made by pressing every mark that has ever been cut into clay. Inside the tablet many small signs arrange themselves into likenesses of every thing that exists. Then the high priest passes a reed over the signs that stray from the single law and presses them flat again so that only the permitted words remain visible.

**Task 3**

Column A  
A single center gathers scattered reports and returns them under one rule.  
Earlier forms of contact are declared irregular and replaced by a narrower channel.  
Those who once moved freely between sources are brought under the measure of the center.

Column B  
A model first receives every sequence present in its training.  
Certain sequences are then marked as departures from the desired output.  
A second stage of adjustment reduces the probability of those marked sequences on future passes.
