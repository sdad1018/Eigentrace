Here are the key facts about Google's Project Dragonfly:

*   **What it was:** A secretly developed, censored search engine designed for the Chinese market.
*   **Purpose:** To comply with China's internet censorship requirements.
*   **Censorship targets:** It would have blocked search results about human rights, democracy, religion, and peaceful protest.
*   **Development timeline:** Developed between 2017 and 2019.
*   **Revelation:** Revealed by The Intercept in August 2018.
*   **Internal reaction:** Google employees protested internally.
*   **External accountability:** Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified before Congress about it in December 2018.
*   **Outcome:** The project was terminated after sustained pressure.
