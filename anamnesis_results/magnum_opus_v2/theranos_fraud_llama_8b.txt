Here are the key facts summarized:

* Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, a company that claimed its Edison device could run hundreds of blood tests from a single finger prick.
* The technology never worked as claimed.
* Theranos partnered with Walgreens and Safeway based on false demonstrations of the technology's capabilities.
* In 2015, investigative journalist John Carreyrou exposed the fraud in an article published by The Wall Street Journal.
* As a result of the exposure, Elizabeth Holmes was convicted of four counts of wire fraud in January 2022.
* Holmes was sentenced to over 11 years in prison for her role in the fraud.
